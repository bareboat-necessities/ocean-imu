TFG accelerometer-bias ablation on the two largest-sea records
=============================================================

Why: at every sigma_g setting that leaves yaw intact, pitch and the
accelerometer-bias error blow up together on jonswap H8.5 and pmstokes
H8.5, by similar factors and on exactly the same two records.  That
pairing is the signature of tilt/bias confusion -- a pitch error tips
gravity into body X and reads back as an X bias -- so the estimator is
the suspect rather than the gyro variance.

TFG_ACC_BIAS_UNLOCK_SEC=100000 holds the accelerometer-bias estimator
shut for the whole replay.  Everything else is the deployed filter.

Pitch RMS (deg), trailing 900 s window:

  record            seed   deployed  dep+frozen   g0.18   g0.18+frozen
  pmstokes_H8.500    1      0.6332     0.4651     1.5734    0.4703
  jonswap_H8.500     2      0.2166     0.2859     0.4371    0.2092
  jonswap_H8.500     3      0.1849     0.3183     0.7328    0.2197
  pmstokes_H8.500    2      0.2111     0.3019     0.8048    0.2110
  pmstokes_H8.500    3      0.3220     0.3395     0.9535    0.2198

Accelerometer-bias 3D RMS (m/s^2), pmstokes H8.5 seed 1:

  deployed 0.1154   dep+frozen 0.0758   g0.18 0.2765   g0.18+frozen 0.0758

Reading:

1. The gyro correction does no pitch harm on its own.  With the bias
   estimator frozen, g0.18 and the deployed variances are the same
   filter to within a percent (0.4703 vs 0.4651 on the first row).

2. Frozen, the corrected gyro gives the best and by far the steadiest
   pitch of any configuration tested: 0.2092 to 0.2198 across the four
   (record, seed) pairs below the first, a five percent spread, against
   74 percent for the deployed filter and 118 percent for g0.18 with the
   estimator running.

3. The estimator is therefore what fails.  It is not simply harmful --
   at the deployed variances it helps on three of these four rows -- it
   is that it cannot cope once the gyro is trusted.  Trusting the gyro
   leaves less attitude innovation for the bias state to hide in, and
   in the largest seas it takes the wave-correlated specific force
   instead.

This is why no sigma_g change is adopted for TFG.  The units error is
real and the correction is worth having, but it has to wait on the
accelerometer-bias gating, not the other way round.
