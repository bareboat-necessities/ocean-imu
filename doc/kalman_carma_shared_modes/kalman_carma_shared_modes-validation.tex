\section{Experimental Objectives}
The standalone shared-modes QMEKF (\texttt{KalmanCarmaSharedModes}, CSM)
promotes Option C of the coupled-prior study: one complexified CARMA(4,1)
mode per axis, driven by a Hermitian excitation estimated from the filter's
own acceleration, plus a coupling confidence that withdraws the coupling,
with a positivity-preserving covariance decoupling, when the evidence fails.
The companion article derives the model. This one asks three questions.
(i) Does CSM reproduce the validated research filter when its confidence is
disabled? (ii) What does the confidence change on the 18 seas of the study,
and in particular on the two the research filter lost (manoeuvring and wave
onset/cessation)? (iii) Does it prevent catastrophic overconfidence when the
coupling prior is wrong, with bounded errors and reasonable uncertainty?
Results of the coupled-prior study are labelled as previously established;
everything else was produced for this article.

\section{Simulation Framework}
\texttt{kalman\_carma\_shared\_modes-sim} has two modes. The \emph{dataset
replay} runs the eight scored records of the pinned dataset through the same
\texttt{W3dSimulationRunner}, seeds, sensor model and adapter configuration
as the CARMA3 replay, with regression bars. The \emph{Monte Carlo mode} runs
the carma3\_bench engine: synthetic directional seas through the benchmark
hull's RAOs, seeded IMU and magnetometer streams, and the same metrics, with
the CSM filters registered in front of the bench's own. Every filter of a row
(the CARMA3 baseline A41, the research filter SHM:est, CSM and its fallback
model CSM:off) sees the same truth and the same sensor stream.

\section{Wave-Data Provenance}
\InputPart{../simulation-dataset.tex-part}
The Monte Carlo seas are the 18 scenarios of the coupled-prior study
(\texttt{tests/kalman\_carma/CarmaBenchTruth.h}): regular waves, PM, JONSWAP
long-crested and spread, swell, short wind waves, mixed, bimodal and
crossing seas, calm water, a still IMU, direction and period changes, onset
and cessation, heading changes, manoeuvring, a very low sea, and magnetic
disturbances with calibration residuals. Ground-truth position, velocity,
acceleration, direction and RAO are used for scoring only. The robustness
injections build their deliberately wrong priors from the true wave
acceleration, and enter the filter as an external prior that it must reject
from its own data.

\section{Sensor-Noise Assumptions}
Accelerometer \SI{1.5}{mg} white with turn-on bias up to \SI{8}{mg} and a
random walk; gyroscope \SI{0.0016}{rad/s} white with bias up to
\SI{0.05}{\degree/s}; magnetometer \SI{0.8}{\micro T} at \SI{25}{Hz} with
hard and soft iron, and in one scenario periodic \SI{15}{\micro T}
disturbances with scale and misalignment residuals; \SI{200}{Hz} with timing
jitter where stated. These are the CARMA3 benchmark's, unchanged.

\section{Baseline CARMA3 Configuration}
A41: the experimental CARMA3 QMEKF with CARMA(4,1) C41J on all axes (21
states), behind the unchanged CARMA3 wrapper (OU-III's startup, magnetic
acquisition, tuner and \SI{0.1}{s} commits). It is verified bit-identical to
the code before the coupled-prior work.

\section{Shared-Mode Configuration}
CSM: 33 states, the estimated coupling (\SI{240}{s} horizon, band
$0.15f_{op}$, both screened on seeds 1001--1020 by the coupled-prior study)
and the coupling confidence with its a-priori defaults (consistency $D^2$
thresholds 6/16, SNR 10--100, yaw rate \SI{3}{\degree/s}, sea change
$0.01$/s, holds \SI{30}{s}, NIS ratio 2/1.5, $\tau=3/60$\,s). CSM:noconf is
CSM with the confidence disabled. It reproduces SHM:est: bit-identical in the
unit test, and equal to float rounding ($5\times10^{-5}$) in the benchmark
rows, where the two objects' memory layouts differ. CSM:off is the coupling
switched off, the independent-axis law of the complexified realization.

\section{Monte Carlo Methodology}
Every row runs one scenario and seed (1--50 final, 1--30 robustness, 1--10
spectra), \SI{1200}{s} unless the scenario says otherwise, scored from
\SI{300}{s} against the wave coordinate. No parameter was chosen on these
seeds.

\section{Statistical Methods}
Changes are $1-\overline{\mathrm{RMS}}_{\mathrm{cand}}/\overline{\mathrm{RMS}}_{\mathrm{A41}}$
over seeds, with 95\,\% intervals from a paired bootstrap that resamples whole
realizations (seeds), never the autocorrelated \SI{200}{Hz} samples. NEES is
that of $[\vct p,\vct v]$ in the filter's own coordinates. Its \emph{mean}
should equal 6 for a consistent filter; the median of a $\chi^2_6$ variable
is 5.35, so both are reported and are not compared with each other. NIS is
the accelerometer innovation's (3 dof). Its absolute level reflects the
inflated measurement noise, so innovation whiteness and cross-axis
correlation are reported as well.
