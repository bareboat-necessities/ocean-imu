\begin{abstract}
This paper studies regional practical stability of the deployed OU--III marine
inertial estimator using its literal 21-state prediction, accelerometer and
magnetic corrections, $S=0$ pseudo-measurements, bias projection, Mahony
frontend, vibration guard, adaptive wave tuning, covariance resets and
scheduler.  The source class is stated explicitly through MARINE MOTION,
SLOW+FAST delivered-IMU and MAGNETIC SERVICE assumptions.  A retained local
real-arithmetic stability theorem is proved on $\sqrt V\le0.15$, together
with the structural prediction, correction, reset, kernel, covariance and
same-history source lemmas required by the outer argument.  The measurement
map possesses a genuine attitude/accelerometer-bias compatibility fibre.
Indefinite STILL therefore requires a compatible-tube target. For MOVING,
the exact same-record pair still requires an all-time magnetic-admission
decision before its absolute-entry target can be ruled out.  The regional
theorem remains open only on the explicitly listed source-uniform release,
magnetic-admission, quotient-coercivity, finite-error, nonlinear and
finite-precision premises.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Accelerometer-bias estimator relations}
The physical SLOW component is carried by one sampled history; the estimator
bias prior does not act on it. The sampled
slow recurrence is
\begin{equation}
 b_{i,s,k+1}=b_{i,s,k}+w_{i,s,k},\qquad
 \norm{w_{i,s,k}}\le\min(2B_{i,s},D_{i,s}\Delta t_k).
 \label{eq:bias-discrete}
\end{equation}
Let $e_b=b-\hat b$. For the accelerometer-bias estimate, define the implemented
prediction factor
\begin{equation}
 \phi_{e,k}=
 \begin{cases}
 1,&\text{H18/held prediction},\\
 \phi_{{\rm OU},k},&\text{A21/active prediction}.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:phi-mode}
\end{equation}
With physical truth $b_{k+1}=b_k+w_k$, both modes obey the single relation
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 e_{b,k+1}^{-}=
 \phi_{e,k}e_{b,k}^{+}+
 (1-\phi_{e,k})b_k+w_k .}
 \label{eq:bias-prediction}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:bias-prediction} is an estimator-mode relation, not a model
imposed on physical truth.
For $z_b=(e_b,b)$ the same identity has the joint form
\begin{equation}
 \begin{bmatrix}e_{b,k+1}^{-}\\b_{k+1}\end{bmatrix}
 =\begin{bmatrix}\phi_{e,k}I&(1-\phi_{e,k})I\\0&I\end{bmatrix}
 \begin{bmatrix}e_{b,k}^{+}\\b_k\end{bmatrix}
 +\begin{bmatrix}I\\I\end{bmatrix}w_k.
 \label{eq:bias-joint}
\end{equation}
The two rows share one physical increment. Neither independent disturbance
columns nor an estimator-induced reset of $b$ preserves this relation.


Measurement correction is carried separately. If the implemented correction
changes the estimate by $\Delta\hat b_k$, then
\begin{equation}
 e_{b,k}^{\rm corr}=e_{b,k}^{-}-\Delta\hat b_k .
 \label{eq:bias-correction}
\end{equation}
For the shipping accelerometer-bias projection,
\begin{align}
 \hat b_{a,k}^{+}&=\operatorname{Proj}_{R_b}
                      (\hat b_{a,k}^{\rm corr}),\nonumber\\
 e_{b_a,k}^{+}&=b_{a,k}-
 \operatorname{Proj}_{R_b}(\hat b_{a,k}^{\rm corr}).
 \label{eq:bias-projection}
\end{align}
Structures preserved: the shipping mode switch between held and active
accelerometer-bias prediction, one shared physical increment for truth and
error, and correction and projection as separate literal operations.
Relaxations introduced: none; these are identities of the implemented
estimator, not bounds.

\section{Proofs of the structural lemmas}
This section records the mathematical arguments used by the machine
certificates.  The certificates check the literal implementation and numerical

\begin{proposition}[OU prediction identities]
For the scalar OU equation $\dot a_w=-a_w/\tau+\xi$, variation of constants
gives $a_w(t+h)=e^{-h/\tau}a_w(t)+\int_0^h e^{-(h-s)/\tau}\xi(t+s)\,ds$.
Integrating successively through $\dot v=a_w$, $\dot p=v$ and $\dot S=p$
gives the shipping coefficients
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \phi&=e^{-x},\qquad x=h/\tau,\\
 \phi_{pa}&=\tau^2(x+\operatorname{expm1}(-x)),\\
 \phi_{Sa}&=\tau^3\!\left(\tfrac12x^2-x-\operatorname{expm1}(-x)\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
The implemented small-$x$ branches are finite truncations, not equalities
to these formulas away from zero. The existing exact-rational source-defect
certificate retains $\Delta\Phi$ and bounds the process difference relative
to the positive ideal OU Gram. Its positive relative margin establishes PSD
for the qualified literal branch; float32 transfer remains separate.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Joseph correction/storage identity]
Let $S=HP^-H^T+R\succ0$, $K=P^-H^TS^{-1}$ and $A=I-KH$.
The Joseph update is $P^+=AP^-A^T+KRK^T$.  Expanding and using
$KS=P^-H^T$ gives $P^+=P^--P^-H^TS^{-1}HP^-$.  Woodbury therefore yields
\[
 (P^+)^{-1}=(P^-)^{-1}+H^TR^{-1}H .
\]
For a homogeneous perturbation $\xi^+=A\xi^-$, the correction loss is the
nonnegative quadratic measurement action.  In the paired covariance--probe
form used later,
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 (\Phi^+)^T(P^+)^{-1}\Phi^+
 &= (\Phi^-)^T(P^-)^{-1}\Phi^-\\
 &\quad -(H\Phi^-)^TS^{-1}(H\Phi^-),
 \qquad\Phi^+=A\Phi^- .
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus the correction cannot be represented by an independently chosen gain or
covariance.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Attitude reset]
The shipping reset acts linearly on error coordinates by a nonsingular block
matrix $G$ and applies the congruence $P\mapsto GPG^T$ to covariance.
Consequently
\[
 (G\xi)^T(GPG^T)^{-1}(G\xi)=\xi^TP^{-1}\xi .
\]
For the implemented attitude block $G_\theta=I+\frac12[d\theta]_\times$,
\[
G_\theta^TG_\theta
=I+\tfrac14(\norm{d\theta}^2I-d\theta d\theta^T),
\]
so its singular values are $1$ and
$\sqrt{1+\norm{d\theta}^2/4}$ (twice).  This proves invertibility and the
reset-conditioning formula used by the interval proof.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Held-BA triangular structure]
During an H18 held-BA segment, BA prediction and correction are disabled.
Hence the BA increment is constant while the remaining state may depend
affinely on it.  In coordinates $(x_{\rm rest},b_a)$ the homogeneous map is
therefore
\[
 \Psi=\begin{bmatrix}A&B\\0&I_3\end{bmatrix}.
\]
The identity BA block precludes strict contraction of the complete H18 state
in a fixed quadratic metric.  Treating held BA as a bounded input and H18 as
a finite bridge avoids that particular contraction obstruction. Uniform
boundedness and finite release still require their separate proofs.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[No-heading-service obstruction]
With no informative heading correction, the axial linearized equations are
$\dot\theta_z=-b_{g,z}$ and $\dot b_{g,z}=0$.  Over duration $T$ their
transition is, up to the sign convention on $b_g$,
\[
 \begin{bmatrix}1&T\\0&1\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Its powers contain a term growing linearly in elapsed time whenever the axial
gyro-bias error is nonzero.  Thus recurring heading information is necessary
for the stated tail theorem.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Active BA uniqueness]
At an active accelerometer correction the BA Jacobian is $J_{ba}=I_3$.

the homogeneous accelerometer equation has the form $r_0+\delta b_a=0$ and
therefore has the unique solution $\delta b_a=-r_0$.  BA cannot contribute a
second independent kernel direction.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Mahony normalization]
Quaternion multiplication by a pure vector is orthogonal to the original
quaternion and has norm $\norm q\,\norm{\omega_c}$.  Hence for the Euler step

\end{proposition}
