\begin{abstract}
This paper studies regional practical stability of the shipping OU--III marine inertial estimator without replacing its deployed prediction, measurement chronology, adaptive tuning, reset, bias handling, or scheduling by a surrogate filter. The proof uses one same physical and sensor history throughout construction, capture, magnetically informed H18, refinement/release, recurring A21, and regional practical stability. Marine motion, magnetic service, and a two-timescale delivered-IMU qualification define the source class. The local real-arithmetic theorem is retained on its qualified region. The outer proof is constructive but not yet complete: a causal interval HistoryCell propagator advances the literal frontend, tuner, mean and covariance from physical input coordinates only, with dependency-preserving normalization and FAST signed-primitive bounds. If an enclosure is unresolved, only physical/sensor history coordinates may be subdivided; generated quantities are never independently split. The remaining obligations include source-uniform finite-error release/recurrence and finite-precision closure. Absolute physical-error entry cannot be imposed during indefinite stillness. An exact moving sensor-compatible pair also defeats the proposed gravity-span/bias-variation separation step; its all-time applied magnetic-service admission remains open. End-to-end shipping stability is therefore not yet claimed.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Accelerometer-bias estimator relations}
The physical SLOW component is carried by one sampled history; the estimator
bias prior does not act on it. The sampled
slow recurrence is
\begin{equation}
 b_{i,s,k+1}=b_{i,s,k}+w_{i,s,k},\qquad
 \norm{w_{i,s,k}}\le\min(2B_{i,s},D_{i,s}\Delta t_k).
 \label{eq:bias-discrete}
\end{equation}
Let $e_b=b-\hat b$. For the accelerometer-bias estimate, define the implemented
prediction factor
\begin{equation}
 \phi_{e,k}=
 \begin{cases}
 1,&\text{H18/held prediction},\\
 \phi_{{\rm OU},k},&\text{A21/active prediction}.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:phi-mode}
\end{equation}
With physical truth $b_{k+1}=b_k+w_k$, both modes obey the single relation
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 e_{b,k+1}^{-}=
 \phi_{e,k}e_{b,k}^{+}+
 (1-\phi_{e,k})b_k+w_k .}
 \label{eq:bias-prediction}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:bias-prediction} is an estimator-mode relation, not a model
imposed on physical truth.
For $z_b=(e_b,b)$ the same identity has the joint form
\begin{equation}
 \begin{bmatrix}e_{b,k+1}^{-}\\b_{k+1}\end{bmatrix}
 =\begin{bmatrix}\phi_{e,k}I&(1-\phi_{e,k})I\\0&I\end{bmatrix}
 \begin{bmatrix}e_{b,k}^{+}\\b_k\end{bmatrix}
 +\begin{bmatrix}I\\I\end{bmatrix}w_k.
 \label{eq:bias-joint}
\end{equation}
The two rows share one physical increment. Neither independent disturbance
columns nor an estimator-induced reset of $b$ preserves this relation.


Measurement correction is carried separately. If the implemented correction
changes the estimate by $\Delta\hat b_k$, then
\begin{equation}
 e_{b,k}^{\rm corr}=e_{b,k}^{-}-\Delta\hat b_k .
 \label{eq:bias-correction}
\end{equation}
For the shipping accelerometer-bias projection,
\begin{align}
 \hat b_{a,k}^{+}&=\operatorname{Proj}_{R_b}
                      (\hat b_{a,k}^{\rm corr}),\nonumber\\
 e_{b_a,k}^{+}&=b_{a,k}-
 \operatorname{Proj}_{R_b}(\hat b_{a,k}^{\rm corr}).
 \label{eq:bias-projection}
\end{align}
Structures preserved: the shipping mode switch between held and active
accelerometer-bias prediction, one shared physical increment for truth and
error, and correction and projection as separate literal operations.
Relaxations introduced: none; these are identities of the implemented
estimator, not bounds.

\section{Proofs of the structural lemmas}
This section records the mathematical arguments used by the machine
certificates.  The certificates check the literal implementation and numerical

\begin{proposition}[OU prediction identities]
For the scalar OU equation $\dot a_w=-a_w/\tau+\xi$, variation of constants
gives $a_w(t+h)=e^{-h/\tau}a_w(t)+\int_0^h e^{-(h-s)/\tau}\xi(t+s)\,ds$.
Integrating successively through $\dot v=a_w$, $\dot p=v$ and $\dot S=p$
gives the shipping coefficients
\[
 \phi=e^{-x},\quad
 \phi_{pa}=\tau^2(x+\operatorname{expm1}(-x)),\quad
 \phi_{Sa}=\tau^3\!\left(\tfrac12x^2-x-\operatorname{expm1}(-x)\right),
 \qquad x=h/\tau .
\]
Their Taylor series at $x=0$ are exactly the implemented small-$x$ branches.
The discrete process covariance is an integral
$\int_0^h\Phi(s)LQ_cL^T\Phi(s)^Tds$ and is therefore positive semidefinite.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Joseph correction/storage identity]
Let $S=HP^-H^T+R\succ0$, $K=P^-H^TS^{-1}$ and $A=I-KH$.
The Joseph update is $P^+=AP^-A^T+KRK^T$.  Expanding and using
$KS=P^-H^T$ gives $P^+=P^--P^-H^TS^{-1}HP^-$.  Woodbury therefore yields
\[
 (P^+)^{-1}=(P^-)^{-1}+H^TR^{-1}H .
\]
For a homogeneous perturbation $\xi^+=A\xi^-$, the correction loss is the
nonnegative quadratic measurement action.  In the paired covariance--probe
form used later,
\[
 (\Phi^+)^T(P^+)^{-1}\Phi^+=(\Phi^-)^T(P^-)^{-1}\Phi^- .
\]
Thus the correction cannot be represented by an independently chosen gain or
covariance.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Attitude reset]
The shipping reset acts linearly on error coordinates by a nonsingular block
matrix $G$ and applies the congruence $P\mapsto GPG^T$ to covariance.
Consequently
\[
 (G\xi)^T(GPG^T)^{-1}(G\xi)=\xi^TP^{-1}\xi .
\]
For the implemented attitude block $G_\theta=I-\frac12[d\theta]_\times$,
\[
G_\theta^TG_\theta
=I+\tfrac14(\norm{d\theta}^2I-d\theta d\theta^T),
\]
so its singular values are $1$ and
$\sqrt{1+\norm{d\theta}^2/4}$ (twice).  This proves invertibility and the
reset-conditioning formula used by the interval proof.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Held-BA triangular structure]
During an H18 held-BA segment, BA prediction and correction are disabled.
Hence the BA increment is constant while the remaining state may depend
affinely on it.  In coordinates $(x_{\rm rest},b_a)$ the homogeneous map is
therefore
\[
 \Psi=\begin{bmatrix}A&B\\0&I_3\end{bmatrix}.
\]
The identity BA block precludes strict contraction of the complete H18 state
in a fixed quadratic metric.  Treating held BA as a bounded input and H18 as
a finite bridge is therefore necessary and sufficient for the later release
argument.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[No-heading-service obstruction]
With no informative heading correction, the axial linearized equations are
$\dot\theta_z=-b_{g,z}$ and $\dot b_{g,z}=0$.  Over duration $T$ their
transition is, up to the sign convention on $b_g$,
\[
 \begin{bmatrix}1&T\\0&1\end{bmatrix}.
\]
Its powers contain a term growing linearly in elapsed time whenever the axial
gyro-bias error is nonzero.  Thus recurring heading information is necessary
for the stated tail theorem.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Active BA uniqueness]
At an active accelerometer correction the BA Jacobian is $J_{ba}=I_3$.
After all other zero-action equations fix a candidate attitude/AW amplitude,
the homogeneous accelerometer equation has the form $r_0+\delta b_a=0$ and
therefore has the unique solution $\delta b_a=-r_0$.  BA cannot contribute a
second independent kernel direction.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Specific-force floor]
The raw delivered specific force is the sum of gravity, physical translational
acceleration and delivered accelerometer error.  The reverse triangle
inequality gives
\[
 \norm{f_{\rm raw}}
 \ge g-A_{\max}-B_{a,s}-B_{a,f}.
\]
With Table~\ref{tab:assumptions}, the right-hand side is strictly positive.
This is a coupled physical norm statement and is not lost merely because an
axis-aligned interval hull contains the origin.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Mahony normalization]
Quaternion multiplication by a pure vector is orthogonal to the original
quaternion and has norm $\norm q\,\norm{\omega_c}$.  Hence for the Euler step
$q^+=q+\frac h2q\otimes(0,\omega_c)$ the cross term vanishes and
\[
 \norm{q^+}^2=\norm q^2\left(1+\frac{h^2}{4}\norm{\omega_c}^2\right).
\]
Therefore normalization is well defined for every nonzero input quaternion.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Completed-square finite-error bound]
For $e_N=Me_0+b$,
\[
V_N=e_0^TM^TJ_NMe_0+2e_0^TM^TJ_Nb+b^TJ_Nb .
\]
Subtract $(1-\gamma)e_0^TJ_0e_0$ and put
$G_\gamma=J_0-M^TJ_NM-\gamma J_0$, $z=M^TJ_Nb$.
If $G_\gamma\succ0$, completing the square gives
\[
2e_0^Tz-e_0^TG_\gamma e_0
\le z^TG_\gamma^{-1}z ,
\]
and hence
\[
V_N\le(1-\gamma)V_0+
b^TJ_Nb+z^TG_\gamma^{-1}z .
\]
This is an algebraic implication only; source-uniform bounds on its
same-history coefficients remain separate obligations.
\end{proposition}

\section{Supporting lemmas}
The labels below are part of the theorem statement.  \textbf{PROVED} means the implication is established under the hypotheses stated here. \textbf{CONDITIONAL} means the algebraic implication is proved but one or more source-uniform premises remain to be certified on the full reachable class. \textbf{OPEN PREMISE} is a required statement not yet proved.  None of these labels promotes an end-to-end theorem.

\begin{lemma}[Shipping OU prediction identities --- PROVED]
For every qualified positive shipping step and applied \(\tau\), the discrete OU coefficients used for \(a_w\), \(v\), \(p\), and \(S\) are the exact integrals of the continuous OU chain, with the shipping small-argument branches equal to their analytic limits.  The associated process covariance is positive semidefinite.
\end{lemma}

For a literal linearized correction with prior \(P^-\), measurement row \(H\), positive innovation covariance and shipping Kalman gain \(K\), the Joseph covariance update is positive definite on the retained domain and its information/storage change equals the correction action generated by the same \(H,R,K\). \end{lemma}

\section{Open theorem dependencies}
The regional theorem is intentionally stated as OPEN until the following
mathematical premises are discharged.  This list is part of the theorem
specification rather than a research chronology.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Finite H18 release.}  Prove source-uniform finite completion of
the literal reference-refinement/release state machine and compactness of its
terminal image.
\item \textbf{Magnetic admission on the planar compatible pair.}  Prove the
all-time applied MAGNETIC SERVICE information floor for the exact MOVING
same-record pair.  Until this is established, the pair is not promoted from a
source-compatible construction to a counterexample satisfying every tail
premise.
\item \textbf{Quiet compatible tube.}  Uniformize the proved quiet source
projection bound in the actual covariance metric and establish boundedness of
the complete shipping execution during arbitrary admitted STILL intervals.
\item \textbf{Quotient/transverse A21 coercivity.}  Formulate the
measurement-compatible gauge explicitly and prove a strict recurring action
lower bound on its transverse complement rather than requiring contraction of
the unobservable physical decomposition.
\item \textbf{Linked finite-error supply and prefix retention.}  Bound the
completed-square forcing, AW/guard source terms, endpoint terms and every
required prefix on one reachable physical HistoryCell; independent extrema of
gain, tuner, covariance and source histories are not admissible.
\item \textbf{Nonlinear and finite-precision transfer.}  Bound the nonlinear
remainder on the retained domain and transfer the real-arithmetic result to
the shipping float32 execution.
\item \textbf{Regime composition.}  Combine construction, H18 release,
qualified MOVING transverse contraction and STILL compatible-tube boundedness
without introducing a new estimator mode.
\end{enumerate}
Failure of an interval enclosure is not a physical counterexample.  Conversely,
an admitted converged physical history that violates a claimed premise must be
reported as a counterexample to that premise.

\section{Conclusion}
The shipping OU--III estimator is analyzed without replacing its state,
adaptive tuning, covariance, correction chronology, bias projection or
scheduler by a surrogate system.  The local real-arithmetic theorem and the
structural lemmas listed above are proved under the stated qualifications.
Absolute convergence of the physical attitude/accelerometer-bias decomposition
is not an admissible global target because the measurement map has a genuine
compatibility fibre.  The regional theorem is therefore formulated through
bounded compatible motion and transverse contraction.  It remains open
precisely on the dependencies listed above; no numerical diagnostic is used
to fill an unproved implication.
