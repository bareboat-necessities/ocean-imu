\begin{abstract}
This companion to the OU--III filter and performance article studies the
stability of the shipping hybrid estimator, not merely a frozen linear model.
We first state why a physical motion class, qualified bias histories and
accepted informative magnetic service are necessary, and exactly which
conclusions depend on them. We distinguish actual Mahony-based startup,
entry into Live, held/active accelerometer-bias modes, gravity-only centre
dynamics, and magnetically informed Live dissipation. The selected alternative
(ALT) proof retains complete same-history finite increments and the full
covariance, while the original P2/P3/P4/P5 route remains independent. Exact
algebraic lemmas, conditional comparison theorems, point diagnostics, and
validated source-uniform certificates have different roles. This study makes
those roles and the remaining obligations explicit; it does not report a
completed shipping startup or end-to-end stability theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope of the stochastic extension}
The deterministic hard-envelope theorem and the following localized stochastic
results are different statements. Unbounded Gaussian noise does not satisfy a
fixed compact pathwise bound for all time merely because its variance is small.
The conditional localization and moment hypotheses below must be proved on
the same accepted/rejected event history; simulation RMS values do not supply
them. They do not remove the open deterministic shipping obligations.
