\begin{abstract}
This paper studies regional practical stability of the shipping OU--III marine inertial estimator without replacing its deployed prediction, measurement chronology, adaptive tuning, reset, bias handling, or scheduling by a surrogate filter. The proof uses one same physical and sensor history throughout construction, capture, magnetically informed H18, refinement/release, recurring A21, and regional practical stability. Marine motion, magnetic service, and a two-timescale delivered-IMU qualification define the source class. The local real-arithmetic theorem is retained on its qualified region. The outer proof is constructive but not yet complete: a causal interval HistoryCell propagator advances the literal frontend, tuner, mean and covariance from physical input coordinates only, with dependency-preserving normalization and FAST signed-primitive bounds. If an enclosure is unresolved, only physical/sensor history coordinates may be subdivided; generated quantities are never independently split. The remaining obligation is a source-uniform finite-error release/recurrence certificate, linked outer-to-inner entry, and finite-precision closure. End-to-end shipping stability is therefore not yet claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Proof scope}
The sole proof path is
\[
\boxed{\text{construction}\to\text{capture}\to H18\to
\text{release}\to A21\to\text{regional practical stability}.}
\]
One execution \(h\) belongs to
\[
h\in{\cal M}_{\rm marine}\cap{\cal B}_{\rm IMU}\cap{\cal S}_{\rm mag}.
\]
The same \(h\) determines physical motion, delivered sensors, frontend and tuner state, covariance, accepted events, scheduler state and estimator mean. Generated filter quantities are functions of this history, not independent theorem inputs. No estimator behavior, tuning law, physical constant, quality gate, measurement cadence, MARINE MOTION condition, MAGNETIC SERVICE condition, or IMU qualification is changed for the proof.

\section{Marine motion}
Let \(p,v,a\in\R^3\), \(R\in{\rm SO}(3)\), and body rate \(\omega\) satisfy
\[
\dot p=v,\qquad\dot v=a,\qquad\dot R=R[\omega]_\times
\]
almost everywhere, with
\[
\norm p\le P_{\max},\quad\norm v\le V_{\max},\quad
\norm a\le A_{\max},\quad\norm\omega\le\Omega_{\max},\quad
\norm{\dot a}\le J_{\max}.
\]
Complete physical stillness may persist arbitrarily long. Otherwise every complete moving window of duration \(T_E\) has gravity-direction span at least \(\theta_E\), and every complete moving window of duration \(T_P\) has displacement diameter at least \(P_E\). The constructive candidate values are
\[
T_E=T_P=30\,{\rm s},\qquad \theta_E=2^\circ,\qquad P_E=0.03\,{\rm m}.
\]
These are qualification values, not inferred sea-state statistics. Physical history continues across proof-word boundaries.

\section{Two-timescale delivered-IMU qualification}
Delivered errors are
\[
e_a=b_{a,s}+b_{a,f},\qquad e_g=b_{g,s}+b_{g,f}.
\]
The slow components obey amplitude and derivative bounds
\[
\norm{b_{a,s}}\le B_{a,s},\quad\norm{\dot b_{a,s}}\le D_{a,s},\qquad
\norm{b_{g,s}}\le B_{g,s},\quad\norm{\dot b_{g,s}}\le D_{g,s}.
\]
FAST components obey instantaneous envelopes and, for every placed
\(0<T\le H_i\),
\[
\norm{\int_t^{t+T}b_{a,f}\,ds}\le\min(B_{a,f}T,C_a),\qquad
\norm{\int_t^{t+T}b_{g,f}\,ds}\le\min(B_{g,f}T,C_g).
\]
The candidate temporal constants are
\[
H_a=H_g=60\,{\rm s},\qquad C_a=0.05\,{\rm m/s},\qquad C_g=0.002\,{\rm rad}.
\]
The primitive history is carried through adjacent proof words and is never reset by the proof.

\section{Dependency-preserving frontend bounds}
Cartesian interval boxes alone lose important algebraic constraints. The proof therefore carries several coupled invariants.

Before vibration conditioning,
\[
\norm{f_{\rm raw}}\ge g-A_{\max}-B_{a,s}-B_{a,f}.
\]
This norm floor is retained separately from XYZ component intervals.

For the Mahony Euler quaternion step
\[
q^+=q+\frac{\Delta t}{2}q\otimes(0,\omega_c),
\]
the exact identity
\[
\norm{q^+}^2=\norm q^2\left(1+\frac{\Delta t^2}{4}\norm{\omega_c}^2\right)
\]
prevents component decorrelation from creating a false zero normalization denominator.

The vibration guard has
\[
f_c=f+w(f_{\rm LP}-f),\qquad0\le w\le1.
\]
Writing \(d_{p,k}=s_{p,k}-f_k\), shipping initialization gives \(d_{p,0}=0\). Smooth physical and slow-bias increments are propagated causally. FAST acceleration is not charged as \(2B_{a,f}\) at every sample. Instead, the complete low-pass-minus-identity kernel is paired with the signed primitive qualification, yielding
\[
\norm{d_{\rm LP}}\le D_{\rm smooth}+D_{\rm FAST}^{\rm Abel}.
\]
If the resulting conditioned-force norm cannot be separated from zero, the leaf is unresolved rather than artificially repaired.

\section{Release and recurring A21}
Release starts from the explicit goLive seed and advances the same mean and covariance history through the literal operation stream. The mean is carried as

Recurring A21 supplies the regular contraction stage after release. Homogeneous contraction alone is insufficient: affine forcing generated by the same physical history must be bounded jointly with the contraction and at every required prefix.

\section{Linked FAST reader}
For a terminal AW reader \(\ell\), a backward interval adjoint is propagated through the literal event stream. At accelerometer sample \(k\),
\[
c_k=\lambda_k^T K_k.
\]
Reverse chronology crosses reset \(G\) before the correction adjoint because forward chronology applies correction then reset. The same-history guard weight is paired samplewise with the reader,
\[
p_k=c_kw_k,
\]
before any Abel variation is taken.

For
\[
Q=h\sum_kc_kw_k[(L-I)u_f]_k,
\]
discrete Abel summation gives, on the qualified FAST horizon,
\[
|Q|\le C_a\left(|q_N|+\sum_j|q_{j+1}-q_j|\right).
\]
Reader and guard-weight interval radii are paired before the first-difference charge; no product of independent global reader and weight suprema is substituted.

\section{Local retained theorem}
The retained local real-arithmetic analysis supplies the inner target
\[
\sqrt V\le0.15.
\]
On this branch the literal OU--III structure, measurement chronology and source assumptions are retained. This local result is used as the target of the outer entry proof; it is not extrapolated globally.

\section{Outer-to-inner entry target}
For a complete finite-error word
\[
e_N=Me_0+b
\]
with storage matrices \(J_0,J_N\), define
\[
G_\gamma=J_0-M^TJ_NM-\gamma J_0,\qquad
z=M^TJ_Nb
\]
and
\[
\chi_\gamma=b^TJ_Nb+z^TG_\gamma^{-1}z.
\]
The required linked entry condition is
\[
\boxed{
\sup_{h\ {\rm reachable}}
\inf_{0<\gamma<\gamma_{\max}(h)}
\frac{\chi_\gamma(h)}{\gamma}<0.15^2=0.0225,
}
\]
together with the corresponding prefix-retention inequalities. The affine vector \(b\) must be generated locally from the literal chronology, not reconstructed from unrelated worst-case source bounds.

\section{Current theorem status}
The following items are established or implemented as proof machinery:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the literal shipping model and single proof path;
\item the MARINE MOTION, MAGNETIC SERVICE and two-timescale IMU source domain;
\item the retained local real-arithmetic theorem;
\item complete-word kernel/compatibility reduction and the same-history physical bridge used by the current path;
\item compact construction/capture/release sets and recurring regular-word machinery;
\item causal HistoryCell generation of frontend/tuner quantities from physical inputs;
\item coupled accelerometer and quaternion normalization invariants;
\item FAST signed-primitive treatment of guard and linked AW source functionals;
\item interval mean/covariance/gain/reset chronology and physical-only subdivision on enclosure failure.
\end{enumerate}

The end-to-end theorem remains open. The decisive remaining tasks are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item complete the physical HistoryCell cover so the causal frontend/tuner and release stream remain source-uniformly enclosed;
\item generate the full literal \((A_k,a_k,b_k,c_k,D_k)\) release recurrence on every verified leaf;
\item certify recurring A21 finite-error contraction and every required prefix;
\item prove the linked outer-to-inner inequality above over the complete reachable cover;
\item close nonlinear remainder, float32 and regime-composition bounds.
\end{enumerate}
A failed interval leaf is not a physical instability result. Conversely, a converged admitted physical leaf that violates a required shipping condition is a genuine counterexample and must be reported.

\section{Reproducibility}
The theorem text is controlled by the literal shipping implementation and generated proof certificates in the repository. Numerical diagnostics are evidence only when their provenance identifies the shipping source revision, preserves the same-history dependency structure, and passes the corresponding fail-closed theorem gate. Research-history notes and superseded proof attempts are intentionally excluded from this article.

\section{Conclusion}
The current proof architecture is shipping-faithful and source-coupled. It retains the adaptive OU--III filter rather than proving stability of a simplified estimator. Local stability and the homogeneous structural ingredients are in place; the remaining work is a constructive source-uniform finite-error release/recurrence certificate, linked entry into the retained local region, and finite-precision closure. Until those obligations are discharged, regional practical stability of the complete shipping estimator remains an open theorem.
