\begin{abstract}
This study analyzes deterministic stability of the shipping OU--III marine
estimator. The marine domain includes locally absolutely continuous physical
acceleration with jerk bounded by $100\,\mathrm{m/s^3}$. A sharp nonuniform
sampling estimate excludes the previously demonstrated 200-Hz ambiguity and
all fixed-attitude stationary-sample aliases of at least six degrees over
32-second windows. A joint three-dimensional vector-information bound
retains accelerometer and magnetic coupling and both magnetic residual
limits. Physical acceleration mismatch contributes a certified supply to
the literal OU prediction in the full covariance metric, without imposing an
OU prior on truth. The complete covariance-energy identity, corrected LIN
path action, joint lower covariance and nuisance upper covariance are
retained. General capture, full corrected-state dissipativity, nonlinear
retention and arithmetic transfer remain unproved.
\end{abstract}

\section{Theorem scope}
Let one physical execution be denoted by $h$. The principal domain is
\begin{equation}
 h\in\mathcal M_{\rm marine}\cap\mathcal B_{\rm IMU}\cap\mathcal S_{\rm mag}.
 \label{eq:intersection}
\end{equation}
The intersection is a same-history requirement. The execution determines
vessel translation, attitude, IMU measurements, physical biases, magnetic
measurements, frontend and tuning state, covariance, event acceptance,
scheduler state, and estimator variables. The proof boundary does not alter
the deployed estimator.

\section{Marine motion}
Let $p(t)\in\R^3$ be wave-induced displacement about a local equilibrium,
$v=\dot p$, $a=\dot v$, with the same history supplying
$R(t)\in{\rm SO}(3)$ and body rate $\omega(t)$. Here $R$ maps
body to world and satisfies $\dot R=R[\omega]_\times$ almost everywhere. Require
\begin{align}
 \norm{p(t)}&\le P_{\max},& \norm{v(t)}&\le V_{\max},\nonumber\\
 \norm{a(t)}&\le A_{\max},& \norm{\omega(t)}&\le\Omega_{\max}.
 \label{eq:pointwise}
\end{align}
Require also local absolute continuity of $a$ and
\begin{equation}
 \norm{\dot a(t)}\le J_{\max}=100\,\mathrm{m/s^3}\quad\text{a.e.}
 \label{eq:jerk}
\end{equation}
This is a selected continuous-history certification limit, not a claim that
every installation is qualified. It caps acceleration change at
$0.6\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$ over the longest admitted $0.006$-s sampling interval.
It imposes no minimum motion amplitude or physical OU prior.
Pointwise bounds are insufficient. Admission additionally requires
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\norm{\int_{t_1}^{t_2}p(\tau)\,d\tau}\le P_{\rm AC},
 \qquad t_2\ge t_1 .}
 \label{eq:pac}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, $\dot q=p$ has uniformly bounded potential differences. The
same potential continues through successive certified superwords; it is not
restarted at word boundaries. A one-time proof-coordinate origin at tail entry
is permissible only as a coordinate transformation of that same continuation.
With the origin fixed at capture, put $S_{\rm true}(t)=q(t)-q(T_c)$.
Consequently $\norm{S_{\rm true}}\le P_{\rm AC}$. The implemented
zero-integral innovation is
\begin{equation}
 r_S=-\hat S=e_S-S_{\rm true},\qquad e_S=S_{\rm true}-\hat S.
 \label{eq:physical-integral-innovation}
\end{equation}
The physical term $-S_{\rm true}$ is therefore retained through every actual
integral correction. Neither $\hat S$ nor the physical potential is reset at
certification or at later superword boundaries.


\begin{lemma}[Permanent displacement DC is inadmissible]
If $p(t)\equiv p_0\ne0$, $v(t)=0$, and $a(t)=0$ indefinitely, then
\eqref{eq:pac} fails.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On an interval of length $T$,
$\norm{\int p\,dt}=T\norm{p_0}\to\infty$.
\end{proof}
Quiet water $p\equiv0$ remains admissible. Finite approximately constant
segments are not excluded. Bounded samples, a finite mean, a spectrum, or
finite-window increments do not prove \eqref{eq:pac}.

Physical CoG translation may be written
\begin{equation}
 p_{\rm CoG}=p_{\rm eq}+p .
\end{equation}
Global origin, current, propulsion, leeway, and secular reference translation
may be represented by $p_{\rm eq}$. If $\ddot p_{\rm eq}\ne0$, however, the
associated specific force remains an implemented model term or a declared
same-history disturbance.

\section{IMU bias}
The total residual accelerometer and gyro biases after the calibration
actually performed are locally absolutely continuous and satisfy
\begin{align}
 \norm{b_a(t)}&\le B_a,& \norm{\dot b_a(t)}&\le D_a,\nonumber\\
 \norm{b_g(t)}&\le B_g,& \norm{\dot b_g(t)}&\le D_g
 \quad\text{a.e.}
 \label{eq:bias-cont}
\end{align}
The constants are qualified separately. No decomposition of physical truth is
imposed: the aggregate residual may simultaneously include turn-on offset,
thermal drift, strain, hysteresis, aging, correlated drift, and other
calibration residuals. The sampled physical histories obey
\begin{align}
 b_{a,k+1}&=b_{a,k}+w_{a,k},&
 \norm{w_{a,k}}&\le D_a\Delta t_k,\nonumber\\
 b_{g,k+1}&=b_{g,k}+w_{g,k},&
 \norm{w_{g,k}}&\le D_g\Delta t_k .
 \label{eq:bias-discrete}
\end{align}
Thus the successor is constrained by its predecessor rather than selected
independently from an amplitude box.

Let $e_b=b-\hat b$. For the accelerometer-bias estimate, define the implemented
prediction factor
\begin{equation}
 \phi_{e,k}=
 \begin{cases}
 1,&\text{H18/held prediction},\\
 \phi_{{\rm OU},k},&\text{A21/active prediction}.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:phi-mode}
\end{equation}
With physical truth $b_{k+1}=b_k+w_k$, both modes obey the single relation
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 e_{b,k+1}^{-}=
 \phi_{e,k}e_{b,k}^{+}+
 (1-\phi_{e,k})b_k+w_k .}
 \label{eq:bias-prediction}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:bias-prediction} is an estimator-mode relation, not a model
imposed on physical truth.
For $z_b=(e_b,b)$ the same identity has the joint form
\begin{equation}
 \begin{bmatrix}e_{b,k+1}^{-}\\b_{k+1}\end{bmatrix}
 =\begin{bmatrix}\phi_{e,k}I&(1-\phi_{e,k})I\\0&I\end{bmatrix}
 \begin{bmatrix}e_{b,k}^{+}\\b_k\end{bmatrix}
 +\begin{bmatrix}I\\I\end{bmatrix}w_k.
 \label{eq:bias-joint}
\end{equation}
The two rows share one physical increment. Neither independent disturbance
columns nor an estimator-induced reset of $b$ preserves this relation.


Measurement correction is carried separately. If the implemented correction
changes the estimate by $\Delta\hat b_k$, then
\begin{equation}
 e_{b,k}^{\rm corr}=e_{b,k}^{-}-\Delta\hat b_k .
 \label{eq:bias-correction}
\end{equation}
For the shipping accelerometer-bias projection,
\begin{align}
 \hat b_{a,k}^{+}&=\operatorname{Proj}_{R_b}
                      (\hat b_{a,k}^{\rm corr}),\nonumber\\
 e_{b_a,k}^{+}&=b_{a,k}-
 \operatorname{Proj}_{R_b}(\hat b_{a,k}^{\rm corr}).
 \label{eq:bias-projection}
\end{align}
For a finite completed injection with $R_b>0$, write
$d_{b,k}=\hat b_{a,k}^{\rm corr}-\operatorname{Proj}_{R_b}
(\hat b_{a,k}^{\rm corr})$. Then
\begin{equation}
 e_{b_a,k}^{+}=e_{b_a,k}^{\rm corr}+d_{b,k}.
 \label{eq:bias-defect}
\end{equation}
\begin{lemma}[Estimate-projection bounds]
For $\norm{b_a}\le B_a$ and an applied positive-radius projection,
$\norm{e_{b_a}^{+}}\le B_a+R_b$. If additionally $B_a\le R_b$, then
\begin{equation}
 \norm{e_{b_a}^{+}}^2+\norm{d_b}^2
 \le\norm{e_{b_a}^{\rm corr}}^2.
 \label{eq:bias-projection-sector}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The first bound is the triangle inequality. The projection variational
inequality gives $(e_{b_a}^{+})^{\mathsf T}d_b\le0$ when $b_a$ lies in
the ball. Expanding $e_{b_a}^{\rm corr}=e_{b_a}^{+}-d_b$ proves the second.
\end{proof}
This Euclidean component inequality is not contraction in a full-state
covariance-weighted metric. Cross terms and all covariance operations remain
in the finite-error argument. A nonpositive configured radius disables the
shipping projection. Moreover, an invalid attitude injection returns before
projection; excluding or enclosing that branch is an arithmetic/domain
obligation. The H18--A21 release preserves nominal bias states but applies
its covariance floor. Any frame change must transport both physical bias and
its estimate before the next prediction.

Physical bias is unchanged by prediction mode, correction, hold/release, or
projection. The shipping gyro-bias mean predictor is identity, giving
$e_{b_g,k+1}^{-}=e_{b_g,k}^{+}+w_{g,k}$ before its actual correction.

\section{Magnetic service}

the ordered differential of the complete preceding same-history shipping
evolution. Let $H_{m,k}$ be the literal magnetic sensitivity and let
$S_{m,k}^{\rm act}$ be the actual innovation covariance presented to the

$W_k^\mathsf{T}W_k=(S_{m,k}^{\rm act})^{-1}$. With $E_{hb}$ injecting
normalized heading and axial gyro-bias coordinates at the window root, define
\begin{equation}
 G_k=W_kH_{m,k}\Phi_{k\leftarrow s}E_{hb}.
 \label{eq:G}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, if $S_{m,k}^{\rm act}=L_kL_k^\mathsf{T}$, one may take
$W_k=L_k^{-1}$. This square-root choice is algebraic only; the information
matrix is built from the same $S_{m,k}^{\rm act}$ that the shipping update
actually factors. Transport may be rectangular at an error-coordinate

a finite-error information margin. For
$T_M<\infty$ and $\mu_M>0$, every certified tail interval must satisfy
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\sum_{k\in\mathcal M[s,s+T_M]}G_k^\mathsf{T}G_k
 \succeq\mu_M I_2.}
 \label{eq:mag-service}
\end{equation}
Only finite, unsaturated, gauged measurements whose shipping correction was
actually applied belong to $\mathcal M$. Attempted callbacks, due events,
packets, rejected or invalid measurements, and saturation do not contribute.
A maximum event gap alone is insufficient because frequent collinear
sensitivities may remain rank deficient.

\section{Necessity of sampling regularity}
\label{sec:capture-obstruction}
\begin{theorem}[Obstruction without the jerk condition]
If \eqref{eq:jerk} is omitted from the point-sample domain, there
are two nonzero continuous histories satisfying the motion and bias bounds
and the actual real-arithmetic magnetic-service condition
\eqref{eq:mag-service}, for which the shipping execution never enters the
physical tilt region $\norm{e_{\rm tilt}}\le\pi/30$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $g=9.80665$, $h=1/200$, $\nu=2\pi/h$, $c=3/5$, $s=4/5$,
and $B=75e_x$. For $\epsilon\in\{-1,1\}$ choose constant body-to-world
attitude and acceleration amplitude
\begin{equation}
 R_\epsilon=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&c&-\epsilon s\\0&\epsilon s&c\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad A_\epsilon=g(0,\epsilon s,1-c)^\mathsf T.
\end{equation}
Define one persistent physical history by
\begin{align}
 a_\epsilon(t)&=A_\epsilon\cos(\nu t),&
 v_\epsilon(t)&=A_\epsilon\sin(\nu t)/\nu,\nonumber\\
 p_\epsilon(t)&=-A_\epsilon\cos(\nu t)/\nu^2,&
 q_\epsilon(t)&=-A_\epsilon\sin(\nu t)/\nu^3.
\end{align}
Then $\dot p=v$, $\dot v=a$, $\dot q=p$ and $\omega=0$ exactly.
All physical residual biases and point-sample sensor residuals are zero.
The acceleration amplitude is $2g/\sqrt5<8.8$. Using only $\pi>3$ gives
\begin{align}
 \norm v&<0.00732,& \norm p&<6.10\times10^{-6},\nonumber\\
 \norm{q(t_2)-q(t_1)}&<1.02\times10^{-8}.
\end{align}
These satisfy the remaining motion limits for every time. Displacement has zero
DC and no physical coordinate is restarted. At every IMU sample $t_k=kh$,
\begin{equation}
 R_\epsilon^\mathsf T(A_\epsilon-ge_z)=-ge_z,
 \qquad R_\epsilon^\mathsf T B=B.
 \label{eq:alias-samples}
\end{equation}
Both histories therefore give identical accelerometer, zero gyro, and
magnetic samples. Supply magnetometer callbacks every eight IMU steps,
the deployed 25 Hz cadence.

The literal wrapper initializes its proxy level. The aligned gravity
average and constant valid north complete the finite gravity and magnetic
acquisition gates. Even if the measured-period gate is unready, the
implemented timeout requires only proxy readiness and the aligned gravity
branch, which hold here. Handoff is level with zero bias and LIN means.
All subsequent innovations are zero; prediction preserves these means,
attitude injection is identity, and projection is inactive. Constant north
also completes the finite reference refinement, with zero yaw gauge change.
The applied-mag count and one-second guard complete active-bias release.
Adaptation can change covariance and weights but not these zero means.
Thus this is the carried construction-to-A21 execution.

At this nominal state $(\theta_z,b_{gz})$ is an invariant source covariance

where $\sigma_g=0.00135$ and $q_g=10^{-10}$. The source zero-rate

The gauged handoff covariance is

\end{proof}

\section{Sampling fidelity and joint vector information}
\begin{lemma}[Sharp nonuniform sampling bound]
For complete cells of lengths $h_k\le h_{\max}$ and total length $T$,
let $\alpha_i$ be the normalized trapezoidal endpoint weights. Then
\begin{equation}
 \norm{\sum_i\alpha_i a(t_i)}
 \le\frac{2V_{\max}}T+\frac{J_{\max}}{4T}\sum_k h_k^2
 \le\frac{11}T+0.15.
 \label{eq:sampled-mean}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Local absolute continuity permits integration by parts on each cell:
\begin{equation}
 \frac h2(a(0)+a(h))-\int_0^h a(s)\,ds
 =\int_0^h(s-h/2)\dot a(s)\,ds.
\end{equation}
The norm is at most $J_{\max}h^2/4$, attained by a triangular tent.
Sum cells, use $\int a=\Delta v$, and divide by $T$. The weights are
$h_0/(2T)$, $(h_{i-1}+h_i)/(2T)$ and $h_{N-1}/(2T)$; they sum to one.
These are proof weights on existing samples, not a replacement integrator.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Exclusion of stationary-looking fixed-tilt aliases]
If true $R$ is constant and every measured accelerometer sample on a
complete-cell window of length $T\ge32$ s equals $-ge_z$, then true tilt is
strictly less than six degrees under the declared residual bounds.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The physical sensor equation gives
$a_i=g(e_z-Re_z)-R(b_{a,i}+n_{a,i})$. Weighted averaging and
\eqref{eq:sampled-mean} imply
\begin{equation}
 2g\sin(\theta/2)\le11/T+0.15+B_a+N_a.
\end{equation}
At $T=32$ the right side is below $1.018917$. Using $\pi>3.14159$
and $\sin x\ge x-x^3/6$ gives $2g\sin(\pi/60)>1.0264$.
Thus $\theta<\pi/30$. The full admitted accelerometer residuals are charged.
\end{proof}
This excludes the entire stated alias class. It does not establish capture
for moving true attitude or for arbitrary records of the adaptive observer.

For constant world field $B$, define $u_i$ as the measured accelerometer
vector transported by true $R(t_i)$ and divided by $g$. Define $b_j$ from
actually applied magnetic measurements similarly, divided by $75$.
Use the preceding $\alpha_i$ and any nonnegative magnetic weights
$\beta_j$ summing to one. True transport is an analytical coordinate, not
an estimator input. With both magnetic residual limits charged,
\begin{align}
 \norm{\bar u+e_z}&\le\epsilon=(11/T+0.15+B_a+N_a)/g,\nonumber\\
 \norm{\bar b-B/75}&\le r=(5+2)/75.
\end{align}
Assume the declared $\norm B\le75$ and horizontal component at least $15$.
Consider the full joint matrix
\begin{equation}
 G=\sum_i\alpha_i[u_i]_\times^{\mathsf T}[u_i]_\times
   +\sum_j\beta_j[b_j]_\times^{\mathsf T}[b_j]_\times.
\end{equation}
Variance positivity implies
$G\succeq[\bar u]_\times^{\mathsf T}[\bar u]_\times+
[\bar b]_\times^{\mathsf T}[\bar b]_\times$.
For any vectors $u,b$, the two smaller eigenvalues of their joint matrix
have sum $\norm u^2+\norm b^2$ and product $\norm{u\times b}^2$;
the remaining eigenvalue equals that sum. Also
\begin{equation}
 \norm{\bar u\times\bar b}\ge1/5-\epsilon-r(1+\epsilon).
\end{equation}
Consequently, on every complete-cell window with $T\ge64$ s,
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{G\succeq\gamma I_3,\quad
 \gamma=\frac{(1/5-\epsilon-r(1+\epsilon))^2}
 {(1+\epsilon)^2+(1+r)^2}>6.29714\,10^{-5}.}
 \label{eq:physical-joint-info}
\end{equation}
No attitude block or cross term is discarded. For a common finite rotation
$Q$ of angle $\varphi$ and unit axis $z$, Rodrigues' formula gives exactly
\begin{align}
 &\sum_i\alpha_i\norm{(Q-I)u_i}^2+
 \sum_j\beta_j\norm{(Q-I)b_j}^2\nonumber\\
 &\qquad=4\sin^2(\varphi/2)z^{\mathsf T}Gz
 \ge4\gamma\sin^2(\varphi/2).
\end{align}
For row-dependent $Q_i$, reverse triangle inequality subtracts the norm of
the stacked variations $(Q_i-Q_{\rm root})u_i$ and their magnetic counterparts
from $2\sqrt\gamma|\sin(\varphi_{\rm root}/2)|$.
These variations and nuisance cancellation still require the actual observer
dynamics. The physical matrix \eqref{eq:physical-joint-info} is not the
nominal innovation-weighted corrected loss in 21 coordinates.

\section{Physical LIN forcing in the covariance metric}
This supplies physical prediction disturbance to the same finite-error
storage; it does not impose an OU law on marine truth. On each cell use the
realized $\lambda=1/\tau_k$ and put $u=\dot a+\lambda a$.
For physical $z=(v,p,S,a)$ the exact identity is
\begin{equation}
 z_{k+1}=F_{\rm ideal}z_k+d_{\rm ideal},\quad
 d_{\rm ideal}=\int_0^h e^{A(h-s)}E_a u(s)\,ds.
\end{equation}
With $W=2\lambda\Sigma_{aw}$, weighted Cauchy--Schwarz proves the
endpoint action inequality
\begin{equation}
 d_{\rm ideal}^{\mathsf T}Q_{\rm ideal}^{-1}d_{\rm ideal}
 \le\int_0^h u^{\mathsf T}W^{-1}u\,ds=:\mathcal A_k.
\end{equation}
The source polynomial branch changes the physical mismatch to
$d=d_{\rm ideal}+(F_{\rm ideal}-F_{\rm src})z_k$.
The existing kernel comparison gives
$Q_{\rm src}\succeq(1-\epsilon_Q)Q_{\rm ideal}$ and
$\norm{(F_{\rm ideal}-F_{\rm src})z_k}_{Q_{\rm ideal}^{-1}}^2
\le A_{\max}^2c_F$; only the acceleration column differs.
Thus, for any $\eta>0$,
\begin{equation}
 d^{\mathsf T}Q_{\rm src}^{-1}d
 \le\frac{(1+\eta)\mathcal A_k+(1+1/\eta)A_{\max}^2c_F}
 {1-\epsilon_Q}.
 \label{eq:physical-action}
\end{equation}
The closed-form branch has zero transition defect. All process cross
covariance is retained. A finite uniform action bound follows from
$\Sigma_{aw}\succeq0.05^2I$, $\lambda\in[1/12,50]$ and
$\norm u\le J_{\max}+\lambda A_{\max}$; convexity puts the maximum of
$(J_{\max}+\lambda A_{\max})^2/\lambda$ at an endpoint.
The certificate uses $\eta=1/100$ and $h\ge0.004$ when summing defect terms.

For the full literal prediction covariance
$P^-=FPF^{\mathsf T}+Q_{\rm total}$, including its actual PSD sync,
the quadratic minimum-energy identity now gives
\begin{equation}
 \fitcol{\norm{Fe+E_{\rm LIN}d}_{(P^-)^{-1}}^2
 \le\norm e_{P^{-1}}^2+d^{\mathsf T}Q_{\rm src}^{-1}d.}
 \label{eq:physical-full-metric-supply}
\end{equation}
Indeed the covariance of a sum minimizes the total root/process quadratic
energy over all decompositions of that sum; choose the displayed $e,d$ and
zero other process inputs. This preserves full root cross covariance and is
an algebraic comparison, not a stochastic hypothesis on the physical motion.
Strict complete-word loss, nonlinear operations, prefix retention and target
arithmetic must still be composed with this prediction supply.

\section{Single shipping proof path}
The requested theorem uses
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
 &\text{construction}\to\text{capture}\to\text{informed H18}\\
 &\quad\to\text{H18--A21 release}\to\text{informed A21}\\
 &\quad\to\text{practical stability}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The physical bias history is continuous through all modes. A successful
capture argument would require, for each execution and initial state,
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{T_c=T_c(h,x_0)<\infty.}
\end{equation}
No common finite startup deadline is assumed. The jerk condition removes
the obstruction of Section~\ref{sec:capture-obstruction}; general capture
under the amended domain remains open, so the tail arguments are conditional. Live intervals preceding the
required magnetic information remain part of the finite pre-certified prefix.
At tail entry the proof inherits, without reseeding, the actual estimator
state, full covariance, bias estimates, physical bias histories, frontend and
tuner state, committed parameters, magnetic reference state, scheduler,
clocks, and physical source continuation.

\section{Necessity of heading information}
On an interval without informative heading observations, heading error and
axial gyro-bias error contain
\begin{equation}
 \begin{bmatrix}\theta_\parallel^+\\e_{b_g,\parallel}^+\end{bmatrix}
 =
 \begin{bmatrix}1&T\\0&1\end{bmatrix}
 \begin{bmatrix}\theta_\parallel\\e_{b_g,\parallel}\end{bmatrix}.
 \label{eq:unipotent}
\end{equation}
Both eigenvalues are one and repeated application gives linear heading growth
when $e_{b_g,\parallel}\ne0$. Hence strict coercive full-state contraction is
not generally available during indefinite loss of informative heading service.
This is a necessity result, not a second stability theorem.

\section{Finite held-bias bridge}
The shipping architecture makes H18 a finite bridge. The external bias hold is
released after magnetic-reference refinement, while the internal gate requires
a finite accepted-update count and a one-second guard. Under
\eqref{eq:mag-service}, at least one informative applied correction occurs in
each service window. Thus, once reference refinement is proved finite, the
count/guard portion of release is finite. H18 then requires finite retention,
not asymptotic contraction.

\section{Complete A21 covariance energy}
Consider the linear comparison along one carried execution and write
$V_k=e_k^{\mathsf T}P_k^{-1}e_k$. Prediction and an applied correction obey
\begin{align}
 P_k^-&=F_kP_k^+F_k^{\mathsf T}+Q_k,\nonumber\\
 S_k&=H_kP_k^-H_k^{\mathsf T}+R_k,\nonumber\\
 K_k&=P_k^-H_k^{\mathsf T}S_k^{-1}.
\end{align}
Here $S_k$ is the actual innovation covariance; a positive-semidefinite
safety increment is included in the effective $R_k$ used by both gain and
Joseph comparison. Frozen gain rows are not regular A21 updates. The update and
$e_k^+=(I-K_kH_k)e_k^-$ give the exact identity
\begin{equation}
 V_k^- - V_k^+
 =(e_k^-)^{\mathsf T}H_k^{\mathsf T}S_k^{-1}H_ke_k^-.
 \label{eq:measurement-energy}
\end{equation}
Prediction contributes the positive-semidefinite loss
\begin{equation}
 B_k=(P_k^+)^{-1}-F_k^{\mathsf T}(P_{k+1}^-)^{-1}F_k\succeq0.
\end{equation}
A nonsingular reset applied by the same congruence to error and covariance
preserves this linear energy. Differences between that comparison and the
finite attitude injection belong to the nonlinear remainder.

Let $M_k$ denote the complete preceding error transport from the word root.
Transport each prediction loss $B_k$ and measurement loss in
\eqref{eq:measurement-energy} to that root and sum them as $D_W$. Then
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{M_W^{\mathsf T}P_W^{-1}M_W+D_W=P_0^{-1}.}
 \label{eq:word-energy}
\end{equation}
Every applied correction and reset is included in $M_k$. Bare kinematic
transport cannot replace it. Rejected measurements contribute no loss.
A sufficient full-state certificate is
\begin{equation}
 D_W\succeq\delta P_0^{-1},\quad 0<\delta<1,
 \qquad \rho_0\le1-\delta.
 \label{eq:full-loss}
\end{equation}
This includes all 21 coordinates and the covariance carried into the word.

\subsection{Loss factors with complete coupling}
Let $P=CC^{\mathsf T}$, $P_0=C_0C_0^{\mathsf T}$ and $T=MC_0$.
For $S=L_sL_s^{\mathsf T}$, a measurement contributes the factor
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal B_m=L_s^{-1}HT,\qquad T^+=T-K(HT).
\end{equation}
It has at most three rows. Orthogonal triangularization of
$[\begin{smallmatrix}R^{1/2}&HC\\0&C\end{smallmatrix}]$ yields lower blocks
$[\begin{smallmatrix}L_s&0\\KL_s&C^+\end{smallmatrix}]$, preserving the exact
Joseph covariance in real arithmetic. This comparison does not replace the
shipping float32 Joseph operation.

For $Q=UU^{\mathsf T}$ with $r$ factor columns, write the full QR array
\begin{equation}
 [FC,U]^{\mathsf T}=[O_1,O_2]
 \begin{bmatrix}L^{\mathsf T}\\0\end{bmatrix},\qquad
 J=\begin{bmatrix}I_n\\0_{r\times n}\end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}
Since $L^{-1}FC=O_1^{\mathsf T}J$, orthogonality gives the prediction
factor $\mathcal B_p=O_2^{\mathsf T}JC^{-1}T$, without subtracting precision
matrices. This admits singular $Q$ whenever the predicted covariance is
positive definite. Stack and compress these factors by QR into $R_W$.
Then, with $Z=C_W^{-1}T_W$,
\begin{equation}
 Z^{\mathsf T}Z+R_W^{\mathsf T}R_W=I,\qquad
 \sigma_{\min}(R_W)^2\ge\delta\ \Longrightarrow\ \rho_0\le1-\delta.
\end{equation}
The magnetometer attitude block is skew-symmetric and has rank two for a
nonzero field. The integrated OU process block can have rank four per axis;
three measurement rows do not make the full covariance rank three.

\subsection{Restricted service and cross terms}
The service premise \eqref{eq:mag-service} controls a restriction to heading
and axial gyro bias. It does not imply an independently embedded full-state
heading observation. For example,
\begin{equation}
 D=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&1\\0&1&0\\1&0&1\end{bmatrix}\succeq0,
 \qquad E_{hb}^{\mathsf T}DE_{hb}=I_2,
\end{equation}
yet $D(1,0,-1)^{\mathsf T}=0$. This matrix is the innovation-energy loss of
a positive-noise Kalman correction: take $P=I_3/10$, measurement rows
$(2,0,2)$ and $(0,2,0)$, and
$R=\operatorname{diag}(16/5,18/5)$, giving $S=4I_2$.
The cancellation direction preserves energy exactly.
This is a counterexample to information lifting, not to a physical marine
execution. Full transported cross terms must be bounded, for example through
a verified Schur complement. A restricted neutral Gramian also cannot supply
a quantitative full-state contraction rate for the stable $a_w$ and $b_a$
coordinates without a compatible comparison.

\section{Matrix LIN covariance comparison}
Partition the state into attitude/gyro bias (AG),
$(v,p,S,a_w)$ (LIN), and active accelerometer bias (BA).
For the linear Gaussian covariance recursion, the endpoint precision quadratic
is the minimum path action with a prescribed endpoint. A trial path beginning
at zero is admissible for every positive initial covariance and eliminates
its initial action. Choose a degree-seven Hermite polynomial for $S(t)$,
with zero initial jets and prescribed terminal $(S,p,v,a_w)$; put
$p=\dot S$, $v=\ddot S$, and $a_w=S^{(3)}$ along this trial path.
AG and BA trial paths remain zero.

For $\lambda=1/\tau$, the process action is bounded using
\begin{equation}
 \int_0^T {\norm{\dot a_w+\lambda a_w}^2\over2\sigma_{aw}^2\lambda}\,dt.
\end{equation}
The cross integral telescopes even when $\lambda$ changes between samples.
All intervening integral and accelerometer observations are charged at their
actual sample times. For a component with $f(0)=0$, disjoint backward cells
of length $h_{\min}$ give
\begin{equation}
 \sum_i |f(t_i)|^2\le {2\over h_{\min}}\int_0^T |f|^2dt
                 +2h_{\min}\int_0^T |\dot f|^2dt.
\end{equation}
Small-argument transition and process-covariance polynomial defects are
included in the same action. Positive-semidefinite covariance synchronization
can only reduce endpoint precision.

With $E_L$ embedding the scaled LIN coordinates, the resulting rational
matrix $A\succ0$ satisfies
\begin{equation}
 E_L^{\mathsf T}P_T^{-1}E_L\preceq A,
 \qquad P_T\succeq E_LA^{-1}E_L^{\mathsf T}.
 \label{eq:lin-path-factor}
\end{equation}
The second inequality retains the full carried cross covariance. It is a
singular full-state bound, not joint AG/LIN/BA coercivity. Separate marginal
floors cannot simply be added. Exact LDL factors verify $A\succ0$; their
smallest pivot is not used as an eigenvalue bound.

The enclosure covers $T\in[16,16.006]$~s,
$\tau\in[0.02,12]$~s, and sample intervals $[0.004,0.006]$~s.
It uses the default spectral-MSE schedule, horizontal process factor one,
$\sigma_{aw}\ge0.05$, accelerometer standard deviation at least $0.05$, and
actual integral standard deviation at least $0.15(0.5)=0.075$.
Alternative user-configured noise policies are not certified by these values.

In scales $(v,p,S,a_w)=(2.4,18,132,4)$, retaining the matrix action and
three raw neutral integral rows gives
\begin{equation}
 J_{S,N}\succeq\mu_{S,N}A_{NN},\qquad
 \mu_{S,N}\ge3.59\times10^{-11}.
\end{equation}
The three event-time intervals are $[0,0.156]$, $[8,8.156]$, and
$[16,16.156]$~s; the endpoint paths have $a_w(T)=0$.
A determinant/adjugate bound encloses all these times with rational arithmetic.
This is a raw translation comparison. It is not $D_W$ in
\eqref{eq:word-energy} and is not assigned to $\rho_0$.

\subsection{Joint post-prediction lower covariance}
At the same endpoint, choose the root after prediction and before the first
current-sample correction. The fresh process covariance gives
\begin{equation}
 P^-\succeq\operatorname{diag}(q_{AG}I_6,0,q_{BA}I_3).
\end{equation}
In attitude/gyro scales $(1,0.02)$, the isotropic gyro term and the
attitude/bias cross-term bound give
\begin{equation}
 q_{AG}=\min\{\sigma_g^2h_{\min},q_gh_{\min}/s_g^2\}
                  -q_gh_{\max}^2/s_g>0.
\end{equation}
The integrated rotation has norm at most $h^2/2$; the looser $h^2$ bound
also covers the small-rate polynomial branch. The positive Simpson
contribution to the attitude diagonal is retained. For active BA, in unit
bias coordinates,
\begin{equation}
 q_{BA}\ge {q_ah_{\min}\over1+2h_{\min}/\tau_{b,e}}>0,
\end{equation}
using $e^{-x}\le(1+x)^{-1}$. Combining this full Loewner comparison with
\eqref{eq:lin-path-factor} by equal convex weights yields
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{P^-\succeq\tfrac12\operatorname{diag}
       (q_{AG}I_6,A^{-1}\otimes I_3,q_{BA}I_3)\succ0.}
 \label{eq:joint-root-floor}
\end{equation}
Both comparisons concern the same carried covariance. No marginal block is
substituted for a full-state inequality. The resulting AG and BA diagonal
bounds are $4.9991\times10^{-10}$ and at least
$4.99999\times10^{-10}$, respectively; the LIN matrix is retained in full.
The bound recurs at regular A21 prediction roots after a complete 16-s
A21 window. A uniform upper covariance bound, every-prefix retention, and
float32 transfer remain separate obligations.

\subsection{Recurring nuisance upper covariance}
The upper comparison can be closed for the 15 coordinates
$n=(v,p,S,a_w,b_a)$ independently of the still-open AG comparison.
In the same default regular A21 profile, let $g_s=0.156$~s and $L=17$~s.
Let $\epsilon_Q$ be the rational relative source process-covariance defect
used in the LIN path action. Define
\begin{align}
 B&=16(1+\epsilon_Q)+16\left(1+{12\over2(0.004)}\right)<156^2,\nonumber\\
 C^2&={(1+\epsilon_Q)1600(0.006)^2 L\over3}<1.
\end{align}
Put $W_v=4/8^2$, $W_{p0}=4(8+g_s)/8^2$,
$W_p=W_{p0}+g_sW_v$, and
$W_S=1+g_sW_{p0}+g_s^2W_v/2$. For $j=1,2,3$, set
\begin{equation}
 E_j={156 L^j\over j!}+{L^{j-1}\over(j-1)!},\qquad
 b_j=E_j+W_j(E_3+100).
\end{equation}
\begin{lemma}[Nuisance upper comparison]
At every operation boundary after 17~s of regular A21 operation,
with inherited default construction/hold/release covariance and no
frame/relock reconfiguration,
\begin{equation}
 P_{nn}\preceq5\operatorname{diag}
 (b_1^2I_3,b_2^2I_3,b_3^2I_3,156^2I_3,I_3/1600).
 \label{eq:nuisance-upper}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The default clamps and convex tuner smoothing give
$\Sigma_{aw}\preceq16I$ and $R_S\preceq10000I$.
The adaptive bandpass noise-floor gain is at most four: for a frozen
coefficient sequence, its first lowpass has unit-white-input standard
deviation at most one, its highpass at most two, and the following lowpass
at most two, by convexity from zero state. Thus the default $0.12$ noise
floor cannot raise the OU standard deviation above four.
The literal sync adds
$\Delta=\Pi_+(\Sigma_{\rm target}-P_{aw})$, so
$0\preceq\Delta\preceq16I$. There is at most one such addition per
prediction. With $\phi=\exp(-h/\tau)$,
\begin{equation}
 \phi^2B+16(1+\epsilon_Q)(1-\phi^2)+16\le B,
\end{equation}
using $(1-e^{-x})^{-1}\le1+x^{-1}$ and
$x=2h/\tau\ge2(0.004)/12$. Construction starts below this bound;
optimal corrections decrease the marginal and attitude resets leave it
unchanged. Likewise the BA variance starts at $0.004^2I$, stays unchanged
while held, and its release floor is that same value. The active BA
process has stationary covariance $I/1600$, which bounds its marginal.

Freeze the realized coefficients and sync increments. Omit acc/mag
corrections in a LIN comparison covariance, retaining S corrections and
adding the \emph{same} realized $\Delta$. Monotonicity of prediction and
S correction, and the decreasing effect of other optimal corrections,
give domination of the actual LIN marginal. No monotonicity of the
positive-part map is asserted. Realize this comparison as an auxiliary
Gaussian linear model; this is matrix algebra, not an independence
assumption about physical adaptive histories.

At target time $T$ choose the latest S observation $t_2\le T$, then the
latest $t_1\le t_2-8$, then the latest $t_0\le t_1-8$.
The source scheduler gives gaps at most $g_s$; hence
$a=t_1-t_0,b=t_2-t_1\in[8,8+g_s]$, $d=T-t_2\in[0,g_s]$,
and $T-t_0<17$. Quadratic interpolation of the three S observations
recovers the entire noise-free neutral state. At $t_2$ its first two rows are
\begin{align}
 L_v&=\left({2\over a(a+b)},-{2\over ab},{2\over b(a+b)}\right),\nonumber\\
 L_p&=\left({b\over a(a+b)},-{a+b\over ab},{a+2b\over b(a+b)}\right),
\end{align}
and $L_S=(0,0,1)$. Propagation by the exact neutral transition to $T$
gives row norms bounded by $W_v,W_p,W_S$. The resulting trial estimator
cancels the arbitrary neutral root and all its cross covariance exactly.

The literal OU input columns satisfy $0\le\Psi_j(h)\le h^j/j!$,
including the small-argument polynomial branch. The neutral semigroup
therefore bounds accumulated AW forcing standard deviation by
$\sqrt B L^j/j!$. For independent fresh neutral process increments,
the driving density is at most 1600 and the transported impulse within
a step of age $s$ is at most $sL^{j-1}/(j-1)!$.
The source comparison $Q_{\rm source}\preceq(1+\epsilon_Q)Q_{\rm ideal}$
and $\sum h^3\le h_{\max}^2\sum h$ bound its standard deviation by
$CL^{j-1}/(j-1)!$. Minkowski combines these contributions without dropping
within-step AW/neutral correlation. Thus each neutral forcing term has
standard deviation at most $E_j$. Interpolation error and observation
noise have standard deviation at most $b_j$. Take AW trial estimate zero;
optimal S-only estimation has no larger error covariance than this trial.
Together with the BA bound, five-block Cauchy--Schwarz proves
\eqref{eq:nuisance-upper}, retaining all nuisance cross terms.
\end{proof}
This is an upper principal-block comparison. It does not supply a full-state
lower comparison or a decay rate. The remaining uniform upper block is AG.

\subsection{Stationary A21 detectability}
Quiet water does not by itself create an unobservable nondecaying mode in
the stationary A21 linearization. To see this, take zero corrected angular
rate, zero nominal wave acceleration, constant attitude and OU parameters,
a fixed sample step $h>0$, and a periodic word of $N$ steps containing applied
S, accelerometer and magnetic observations. Assume gravity and field are
nonparallel. The only prediction eigenvalues are $1$, $e^{-h/\tau_{aw}}$
and $e^{-h/5000}$. If $F^Nx=x$, the stable AW/BA components vanish,
the attitude/gyro block forces $b_g=0$, and the neutral integrator block
forces $v=p=0$. Thus $x=(\theta,0,0,0,S,0,0)$ is unchanged at every event.
A zero S output forces $S=0$; zero acc/mag outputs force both
$[g_b]_\times\theta=0$ and $[B_b]_\times\theta=0$, hence $\theta=0$.
An unobservable generalized unit-eigenspace would contain such an
eigenvector, so the lifted pair is detectable. A lever-arm gyro Jacobian
cannot change this conclusion because $b_g=0$ already.

This proof concerns the stationary linear comparison, not arbitrary
quiet-driven nonlinear trajectories or capture. The physical bias remains
independent of the estimator OU prior and contributes its exact mismatch.
Replacing active BA decay by a held identity predictor would change the
argument. There is no demonstrated quiet-water instability here warranting
its exclusion. If varying histories require extra motion excitation, it
must be a quantitative every-window condition: excluding only exactly zero
amplitude does not yield a uniform information margin.

\section{Finite-error regional theorem obligations}
After \eqref{eq:full-loss} is certified uniformly, let the complete nonlinear
word remainder have storage-norm Lipschitz bound $\eta(r)$ on a retained
region. The sufficient finite-error condition is
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\rho=(\sqrt{\rho_0}+\eta(r))^2<1.}
 \label{eq:a21-small-gain}
\end{equation}
For a smooth remainder with $\eta(r)\le L_2r$, a strict radius must satisfy
$r<(1-\sqrt{\rho_0})/L_2$. The bias projection sector
\eqref{eq:bias-projection-sector} must be composed in the same storage;
Euclidean dissipation of one component does not control covariance cross
terms. Projection-inactive release is not assumed.

Bounded physical, model, and arithmetic forcing then requires an independently
certified supply bound in
\begin{equation}
 V_{j+1}\le\rho V_j+c_d\bar d^2,\qquad 0\le\rho<1.
 \label{eq:diss}
\end{equation}
Uniform coercivity and every-prefix bounds and forward retention require
\begin{equation}
 m\norm{e_j}^2\le V_j\le M\norm{e_j}^2.
\end{equation}
If these bounds and finite entry are established, the ultimate root-error
bound is $\limsup_j\norm{e_j}\le
\sqrt{c_d/[m(1-\rho)]}\,\bar d$, with a separate prefix gain.
Float32 roundoff is additive supply; kernel bounds use
$\gamma_n=nu/(1-nu)$, $u=2^{-24}$, before composing actual operations and
magnitude bounds. This implication is conditional until full loss,
covariance coercivity, nonlinear radius, supply, capture, bridge/release, and
prefix certificates close. Finite simulations do not establish these
source-uniform inequalities.

\section{Evidence boundary}
Exact rational checks establish the LIN matrix action, the joint
post-prediction lower covariance, the complete linear
energy identity, and the failure of restricted-information lifting. These
results do not establish a source-uniform full-state contraction factor.
For a factor partition $[B_h,B_n]$, exact nuisance elimination gives
\begin{equation}
 \min_n\norm{B_hh+B_nn}^2
 =\norm{(I-\Pi_{\operatorname{range}(B_n)})B_hh}^2.
\end{equation}
An independent-column range basis handles singular nuisance blocks; a
positive reduced heading matrix still leaves any nuisance nullspace open.
Universal six-degree physical capture is refuted by
Section~\ref{sec:capture-obstruction}. General magnetic-reference completion,
H18 and release retention, recurring service, physical qualification,
nonlinear storage and target arithmetic remain unproved. Numerical certificate reproduction does not imply
end-to-end proof completion.

\section{Conclusion}
The stability problem is posed as one same-history theorem with continuing
magnetic information. The formulation excludes permanent wave-coordinate
offsets without excluding quiet water, treats total residual bias independently
of estimator priors, and uses one prediction equation across held and active
accelerometer-bias modes. End-to-end regional practical stability remains
unproved. The adopted physical jerk condition excludes the old sampling
ambiguity and yields quantitative sampling fidelity, joint vector information
and full-metric physical prediction supply. The remaining bridge is the
actual corrected 21-state loss, followed by capture, nonlinear retention and
arithmetic transfer on the same persistent shipping execution.
