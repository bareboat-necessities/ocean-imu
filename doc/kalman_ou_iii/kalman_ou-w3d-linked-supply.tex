\begin{abstract}
An exact complete-word inequality retains the direction of physical and
implementation forcing relative to the same covariance-metric loss, instead
of multiplying separate worst-case gain and contraction bounds. An 80-digit
carried-word diagnostic improves the separated bound but fails the proposed
inner-radius budget. A distinct exact residual construction demonstrates that
true roll/pitch excitation does not guarantee full physical-error entry below
$\sqrt V=0.15$ under the stated norm-only sensor-residual qualification.
The conditional local theorem is retained; no outer entry or end-to-end
stability certificate is promoted.
\end{abstract}

\section{Physical qualification and proof scope}
EXCITED\_MOVING is sustained vessel motion in which the vessel undergoes a
non-negligible change in roll or pitch within a bounded time. For true
body-to-world rotation $R_{bw}$, let $u_g=R_{bw}^{\mathsf T}g_0/\norm{g_0}$.
Fixed physical constants $T_X,\theta_X>0$ require
\[
 \sup_{s,t\in W}\arccos\bigl(u_g(s)^{\mathsf T}u_g(t)\bigr)\ge\theta_X
\]
for every complete $T_X$ window in an excited episode. This is not an
estimator-dependent switch; weak motion remains distinct. Sensor-bias and
residual sufficiency inequalities are separate proof obligations.

The homogeneous comparison action is not the energy of the base innovations.
In particular $\mathcal D_W=0$ does not imply $r_a^{\rm base}=r_m^{\rm base}
=r_S^{\rm base}=0$, and therefore does not imply a zero nominal AW mean.
The previous four-zero Rolle argument cannot be applied to that base history;
the source small-argument OU transition polynomials are also not exact
continuous exponential integrals. The alleged outer zero-action closure is
withdrawn. Also, pre-Live zero means are not A21 release means after H18 has
run. Finally $P\succeq P_{\min}$ is insufficient for compactness of
$e^{\mathsf T}P^{-1}e\le C$: $P=n^2,e=n$ is a scalar counterexample.

\section{Linked complete-word inequality}
Freeze coefficients along one literal physical/filter execution and write
\[
 e_i=A_i e_{i-1}+d_i,\qquad
 e_N=M e_0+b,\qquad
 b=\sum_i A_N\cdots A_{i+1}d_i .
\]
All physical, nonlinear, projection, reference and arithmetic defects remain
in the signed sum. Set $J_0=P_0^{-1}$ and $J_N=P_N^{-1}$.
\begin{theorem}[Sharp linked additive constant for a fixed word]
Let $0<\gamma<1$ and suppose
\[
 G=J_0-M^{\mathsf T}J_NM-\gamma J_0\succ0.
\]
Define
\[
 z=M^{\mathsf T}J_Nb,\qquad
 \chi_\gamma=b^{\mathsf T}J_Nb+z^{\mathsf T}G^{-1}z.
\]
Then
\begin{align*}
 V_N&=(1-\gamma)V_0+\chi_\gamma
 -(e_0-G^{-1}z)^{\mathsf T}G(e_0-G^{-1}z),\\
 \sqrt{V_N}&\le\sqrt{(1-\gamma)V_0+\chi_\gamma}
 \le\sqrt{1-\gamma}\sqrt{V_0}+\sqrt{\chi_\gamma}.
\end{align*}
For fixed $M,b,J_0,J_N,\gamma$, $\chi_\gamma$ is the smallest additive
constant valid for all unrestricted root errors $e_0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Expand $(Me_0+b)^{\mathsf T}J_N(Me_0+b)$ and complete the square in $e_0$.
The subtracted quadratic is nonnegative and vanishes at $e_0=G^{-1}z$.
\end{proof}
The statement is pointwise valid when $b$ depends on the actual error and
history. A source-uniform theorem still requires a bound over linked
reachable pairs $(M,b)$, not independent selections of their factors.

With $P_0=L_0L_0^{\mathsf T}$, $P_N=L_NL_N^{\mathsf T}$,
$H=L_N^{-1}ML_0$, $\beta=L_N^{-1}b$ and $\Delta=I-H^{\mathsf T}H$,
\[
 \chi_\gamma=\norm\beta^2+
 (H^{\mathsf T}\beta)^{\mathsf T}(\Delta-\gamma I)^{-1}
 H^{\mathsf T}\beta.
\]
Equal forcing norms can have very different costs: for
$H=\operatorname{diag}(.99,.5)$ and $\gamma=.01$, unit forcing in the first
and second coordinates gives $\chi=100$ and $\chi=99/74$, respectively.

\section{Retention and finite entry}
Suppose, on a proved retained history class, $\gamma\ge\gamma_*>0$ and
$\chi_\gamma\le\chi_*$. Put $r_*^2=\chi_*/\gamma_*$. Then
\[
 V_n\le r_*^2+(1-\gamma_*)^n(V_0-r_*^2).
\]
Root retention in $V\le C$ needs $\chi_*\le\gamma_*C$.
Finite entry into $V<r_{\rm in}^2$ needs the strict inequality
$r_*<r_{\rm in}$. Equality may give only asymptotic approach.
For each prefix its own $M_\ell,b_\ell,J_\ell$ must satisfy a linked bound;
endpoint contraction alone does not prove every-prefix retention.
For fixed prefix data, one sufficient test is existence of $\mu_\ell\ge0$
with
\[
\begin{bmatrix}
 \mu_\ell J_0-M_\ell^{\mathsf T}J_\ell M_\ell &-M_\ell^{\mathsf T}J_\ell b_\ell\\
 -b_\ell^{\mathsf T}J_\ell M_\ell &C_{\rm pre}-b_\ell^{\mathsf T}J_\ell b_\ell-\mu_\ell C
\end{bmatrix}\succeq0.
\]
No source-uniform or prefix certificate is asserted here.

\section{Exact finite-residual obstruction}
\begin{proposition}[Excited true motion with quiet packets]
Under the norm-only bounds $\norm{n_a}\le.3$ m/s$^2$ and
$\norm{n_g}\le.02$ rad/s, the suggested $1^\circ/60$ s EXCITED\_MOVING
qualification does not imply eventual retained full-error $V\le.15^2$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $\phi(t)=.01\sin(t/2)$, $R_{bw}=R_x(\phi)$, $B=75e_x$ and
$p=v=a=S_{\rm true}=0$. Choose constant $b_a=.01e_x$, $b_g=0$ and
\[
 n_g=-\dot\phi e_x,\qquad
 n_a=gR_{bw}^{\mathsf T}e_z-g_{\rm model}e_z-.01e_x,
\]
where physical $g=9.80665$ is distinct from the literal parameter
$g_{\rm model}=9.8066501617431640625$.
The measured packets are exactly $\omega_m=0$, $f_m=-g_{\rm model}e_z$ and
$m_m=75e_x$. Bias rates, translation, jerk and primitive are zero, and
\[
 \norm{n_a}<.108067<.3,\qquad \norm{n_g}\le.005<.02.
\]
Every 60-s interval contains a full $4\pi$-s period and has true tilt span
$.02$ rad, exceeding one degree. The literal filter, Mahony proxy, tuner and
reference follow the existing quiet packet history. Actual innovations vanish
by this explicit induction, not by an inference from homogeneous action.
The quiet actual magnetic-service lower bound $3.720373823\ldots$, multiplied
by $(1-.01^2/2)^2$ for the true-axis projection, remains above one.
Nominal BA is zero; the inherited $P_{b_ab_a}\preceq I/1600$ gives
\[
 V\ge e_{b_a}^{\mathsf T}P_{b_ab_a}^{-1}e_{b_a}
 \ge(40\times.01)^2=.16,\qquad \sqrt V\ge.4>.15.
\]
The active BA mean mismatch exactly preserves this physical error:
$\phi_b c+(1-\phi_b)c=c$. All-time float32 totality is not claimed.
\end{proof}
This is a deterministic norm-only construction, not a claim of typical
sensor behavior. A temporal or stochastic residual restriction excluding it
would be a different qualification. None is introduced. The conditional local
homogeneous theorem is not refuted, and filter divergence is not claimed.
