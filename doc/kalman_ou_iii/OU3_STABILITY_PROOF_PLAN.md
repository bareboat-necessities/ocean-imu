# OU-III three-mode directional-sea stability proof plan

## Research question

Can the OU-III stability theorem be certified on a sound outer enclosure of physically
admissible, oscillatory, multimodal directional sea states instead of on every motion
allowed by independent instantaneous source bounds, while retaining the exact deployed
tuner, scheduler, covariance updates, and hybrid events?

## Hypothesis and falsification

**Hypothesis.** A source-generated three-partition directional-sea language, with
`M_max = 3`, is a sound refinement of the existing
`OU3_P2_CORRELATED_STAGE_TRANSFER_V1` language and removes physically impossible
sample-to-sample source/tuner combinations without replay fitting; finite-window P3/P4
contraction can therefore be sought on that refined language without changing the filter.

The hypothesis is falsified if any of the following occurs:

1. an execution satisfying the declared sea/RAO/rate assumptions is not enclosed by the SEA3 language;
2. the SEA3 language contains transitions that cannot be embedded into the current implementation-source language;
3. exact WavePeriodEstimator, variance-EMA, parameter-EMA, stage/commit, or pseudo-update scheduler semantics are lost in the refinement;
4. the resulting P3 finite-window information margin is non-positive;
5. the P4 nonlinear lifted dissipation margin is non-positive on every useful funnel; or
6. P5 cannot certify finite capture from the declared 45-degree entrance.

This PR only installs the theorem, plan, clamp-parity checks, and non-promoting screening
estimates. It does **not** claim that items 1--6 have already been proved.

## Target theorem

The target result is **uniform regional practical stability with finite capture over
three-mode directional sea states**.

For at most three active directional partitions,

\[
E(\omega,\theta;\lambda)
=
\sum_{r=1}^{3}
S_J(\omega;H_r,T_{p,r},\gamma_r)
D(\theta;\beta_r,s_r,\omega),
\]

with inactive slots represented by \(H_r=0\), PM represented by \(\gamma_r=1\), and
the total-energy constraint

\[
H_s^2=\sum_{r=1}^{3} H_r^2.
\]

The admissible class includes single-system seas, wind sea plus swell, crossing seas,
and three-system multimodal seas. The sea parameters are allowed to evolve only through
a declared compact rate-bounded transition relation. The directional spectrum is passed
through a certified vessel/IMU response before it reaches the proof source; independent
axis extrema are not substituted for this vector process.

Let the resulting source be

\[
u_k=u^s_k+d^s_k,
\]

where \(u^s\) is generated by the admissible sea model and \(d^s\) is the bounded
out-of-class/model-mismatch input. The exact deployed tuner and scheduler are part of the
augmented source state. After P5 capture, the desired lifted inequality is

\[
V_{k+N_W}
\le
\rho V_k
+\gamma_s D_{s,k}
+\gamma_n D_{n,k},
\qquad \rho<1,
\]

on a finite sea window \(N_W\). A common wave period is deliberately not assumed because
the three active systems can be incommensurate.

## Soundness relation

The central language contract is

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{actual\ sea}}
\subseteq
\widehat{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{SEA3}}
\subseteq
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{current\ source}}.
\]

The existing arbitrary implementation-source proof route remains available as the
stronger diagnostic envelope. `OU3_P2_CORRELATED_STAGE_TRANSFER_V1` is not silently
redefined. SEA3 will introduce a new interface only after the two inclusions above are
machine checked.

## Certificate chain

### SEA0 -- directional sea admissibility

Build a source-generated, trajectory-independent enclosure for `M_max = 3`.

Required deliverables:

- compact bounds for each partition's \(H_r,T_{p,r},\gamma_r,\beta_r,s_r\);
- explicit rate bounds for slow sea evolution;
- normalized directional spreading law and JONSWAP/PM spectral family;
- total-energy coupling across partitions;
- a bounded directional vessel/IMU response (RAO) or a conservative response family;
- a finite-dimensional oscillator/shaping realization, or a hard finite-window dynamic IQC that outward-encloses it;
- hard finite-window energy/phase constraints suitable for deterministic certification;
- a separate stochastic corollary for random sea realizations rather than an impossible infinite-time pointwise Gaussian bound.

**Promotion condition:** no SEA0 result may promote until the declared physical domain,
rate bounds, and response enclosure are source/reference justified and outward checked.

### P1 -- theorem operating branch

Retain the current startup/Normal-Live/hybrid contract and bind it to SEA0.

In particular:

- 200 Hz nominal theorem schedule;
- zero lever arm in the current proof branch;
- dormant/transparent vibration-guard theorem branch;
- impacts/slams outside the theorem branch;
- 45-degree attitude entrance;
- every valid IMU sample in Normal Live executes the accelerometer update;
- no accelerometer rejection is admitted in Normal Live;
- magnetic/regauge, bias release, tilt reset/relock, cooldown, and covariance-sync events remain explicit hybrid obligations.

P1 must not turn these conditions into claims about product behavior outside the declared branch.

### P2 -- SEA3 reachable source/tuner language

Augment the current correlated stage-transfer state with enough sea memory to preserve:

- oscillator phase/frequency continuity;
- direction and cross-axis vector correlations;
- all three modal amplitudes and the total-energy coupling;
- slow parameter motion;
- WavePeriodEstimator state;
- band-variance EMA state;
- parameter EMA state;
- \(R_S\) smoothing memory;
- stage/commit history;
- pseudo-update scheduler phase.

The SEA3 graph is a refinement, not a Cartesian grid over independent
\(\tau,\sigma_{aw},R_S\).

**Required machine check:**

\[
\widehat{\mathcal L}_{\mathrm{SEA3}}
\subseteq
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{OU3\_P2\_CORRELATED\_STAGE\_TRANSFER\_V1}}.
\]

Replay traces may falsify this enclosure but may not define it.

### P3 -- finite-window linear information/covariance certificate

Choose a finite sea window \(T_W=N_Wh\) large enough to contain the required information
from every active partition. Do not require a common modal period.

Prove, for both held-bias and active-bias fixed dimensions,

\[
\mathcal O_{k,N_W}\succeq\alpha_O I,\qquad \alpha_O>0,
\]

and outward covariance/process bounds on the same SEA3 history. Then prove the existing
information-metric word contraction, or an equivalent full-matrix lifted contraction,
over SEA3-complete windows.

The current canonical P3 usefulness gate stays mechanism-independent unless numerical
evidence shows that a separate published threshold is mathematically required.

### P4 -- nonlinear lifted dissipation and inner invariant funnel

Reuse the exact Cayley/source-operation machinery, but replace arbitrary-source words by
SEA3-complete finite windows. Preserve exact shipping operation order, immediate
quaternion/covariance resets, full attitude--translation cross terms, and exact
time-varying tuner/scheduler behavior.

Target:

\[
V_{k+N_W}-\rho V_k
\le
\gamma_sD_{s,k}+\gamma_nD_{n,k},
\qquad \rho<1,
\]

with every prefix remaining inside the declared chart/source domain. No one-sample
decrease is required.

### P5 -- finite capture

Keep capture separate from the inner P4 argument. From the declared 45-degree entrance
set, prove

\[
e_{k_0}\in\mathcal E_{45}
\Longrightarrow
\exists K\le K_{\rm cap}:
e_{k_0+K}\in\mathcal F_{\rm inner},
\]

and show that the P4 window map leaves \(\mathcal F_{\rm inner}\) invariant.

Only after this step may the article state the finite-capture conclusion without a conditional qualifier.

## Initial non-promoting sizing estimates

`tools/ou3_sea3_initial_estimates.py` reports deterministic screening numbers derived from
the current source clamps. With \(h=5\) ms and
\(0.03\le f_{\rm tune}\le1.2\) Hz:

- one oscillator component advances only \(0.054^\circ\) to \(2.16^\circ\) per IMU sample;
- over the maximum 150 ms pseudo-update gap it advances \(1.62^\circ\) to \(64.8^\circ\);
- the maximum pseudo-update gap is 30 IMU samples;
- the tune-period envelope is approximately 0.833 s to 33.333 s;
- using the WavePeriodEstimator's current four-period, 20--180 s moment-horizon rule only as a sizing proxy gives 20--133.333 s, or about 4,000--26,667 IMU samples;
- a rational shaping approximation with 2, 4, or 6 oscillator pairs per directional partition would contain 12, 24, or 36 sea-shaping states before tuner/scheduler augmentation.

These numbers are **not certificates**. In particular, the final \(T_W\) must come from
the certified directional sea/RAO enclosure and finite-window observability requirement,
not from the tuning clamp.
