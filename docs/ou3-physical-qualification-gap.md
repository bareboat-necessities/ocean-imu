# Physical qualification gap for the final A21 entry margin

The repository was audited for evidence that can numerically instantiate H_a, C_a, H_g, C_g, T_E, theta_E, T_P and P_E.

The BMI270 low-frequency residual qualification document is a PROTOCOL, not a completed qualification. It requires assembled-device captures against independent specific-force/rate truth, reference uncertainty, long stationary records, slow dynamic sweeps, repeatability and published raw-data hashes. No passing artifact with the required deterministic temporal residual envelope is committed. Its 60-s / 1-degree values are explicitly laboratory/proof targets, not adopted deployment constants.

Calibration CSVs cannot substitute: fit/hold data do not measure delivered residual against independent dynamic truth on the required 10--100-s scale. Synthetic IMU seeds and replay noise are not physical qualification.

On MARINE, simulations/RAOs and finite carried wave histories exist, but there is no certified all-complete-window minimum attitude-direction and displacement diameter for a declared deployment class. Simulation examples cannot establish deterministic T_E, theta_E, T_P or P_E guarantees.

Therefore all eight constants remain OPEN and chi_*/delta_ann cannot presently be evaluated numerically without inventing physical evidence.

The mathematical test is ready. Given qualified constants, construct one carried reachable class with the same slow predecessors, fast primitives, MARINE p/v/a/S history, actual gyro transport, applied magnetic service and literal tuner/covariance chronology. Evaluate linked chi_* and homogeneous annulus loss delta_ann on that SAME compact class. Finite entry requires the strict linked-supply reserve; equality is insufficient.

Required evidence:
1. assembled AtomS3R/BMI270 calibrated residual captures against independent acceleration/rate truth sufficient to certify H_a,C_a,H_g,C_g;
2. vessel/deployment records or a declared physical certification envelope proving all-window attitude and displacement spans.

Until those artifacts exist, theorem status is OPEN, not failed.
