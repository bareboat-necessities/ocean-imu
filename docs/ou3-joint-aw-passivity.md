## 1. Correction to the tempting zero-mean argument

The statement

`mean(a_phys)=0 => mean(a_hat_w)=0`

is not an exact property of shipping OU-III. The estimator is a chronological,
time-varying Kalman recursion. Its gains and OU/pseudo-measurement schedule are
generated by the same adaptive sea-state history. A zero-mean physical input
can therefore have a nonzero gain-weighted mean (phase-locked rectification).

The existing carried AW audit already demonstrates this mechanism: the signed
16 s tracking-error mean reached 0.371 m/s^2 on admitted test histories even
though the relevant physical acceleration mean is small/zero. Therefore a
proof based only on the sign of K_aw,a, or on an LTI/DC intuition, is invalid.

This does NOT rescue the H18/A21 compatibility pathology. The pathological
tilt/BA compatibility needs a transverse nominal AW mean on the gravity-field
scale (the current Corollary A* threshold is 1.96133 m/s^2, and the
inclination-domain gravity projection is about 1.70 m/s^2 at the worst allowed
80 degree inclination). The useful problem is to prove a source-uniform upper
bound on shipping rectification below that threshold.

## 2. The three tuning quantities must remain coupled

For the deployed OU-III wrapper, the default r_S law is SpectralMSE. At the
unclamped operating point,

    tau = c_tau/(2 f),
    sigma_aw = c_sigma sigma_a,B,
    T_S = c_T tau,

and the pseudo-measurement standard deviation is

    r_S = C_J (2 r_a)^(1/14)
              sigma_a,B^(6/7)
              tau^(24/7) / sqrt(T_S).

Thus r_S is not an independent nuisance parameter. With T_S proportional to
tau,

    r_S proportional to sigma_a,B^(6/7) tau^(41/14),

and the continuous-equivalent S-information rate is

    I_S = 1/(r_S^2 T_S)
        proportional to sigma_a,B^(-12/7) tau^(-48/7).

The proof must use tau, sigma_aw, r_S and T_S as one reachable schedule,
including their clamps, smoothers, delayed commits, and pending AW covariance
synchronization. Independent boxes for these quantities admit covariance/gain
chronologies that shipping cannot generate.

## 3. Why a sign proof for P_aw,S is not the right theorem

Prediction through the OU-integrator chain creates the expected positive
same-axis a_w-to-S covariance from fresh process noise. But accelerometer
corrections, S corrections, attitude resets, BA coupling, anisotropic/correlated
AW covariance and synchronization all alter cross-covariances.

More importantly, even if K_aw,S has the restoring sign, S=0 is a
pseudo-measurement, not a physical measurement of the vessel's true integral
displacement. In an estimation-error storage relative to physical truth, its
innovation contains a physical-source term. Hence it is not legitimate to
declare the S update unconditional physical-error dissipation merely because a
Kalman correction decreases covariance-weighted homogeneous error.

The robust target is a complete-cycle input/output or dissipativity bound.

## 4. Exact chronological AW identity to exploit

Write the literal AW mean update over operation i as

    a_{i+1} = Phi_i a_i
              + K^a_acc,i r_acc,i
              - K^a_S,i S_i
              + d_i,

where d_i contains only explicitly enumerated shipping operations/defects
(reset-induced coupling, parameter commit/sync chronology, arithmetic terms,
and any enabled auxiliary update). Substituting

    r_acc,i = R_hat_i(a_phys,i - a_i)
              + r_att,i + r_BA,i + r_sensor,i

gives a closed same-history recursion. Iteration over a complete word W gives

    a_N = M_W a_0
          + sum_i G_i a_phys,i
          + sum_i H_i r_att/BA/sensor,i
          + sum_i J_i S_i
          + d_W.

The coefficients M_W,G_i,H_i,J_i are not free gains: they are generated by the
literal reachable Riccati/tuner chronology.

For any fixed transverse world direction u, define the signed normalized word
functional used by Corollary A*

    mu_W = sum_i alpha_i u' a_i,
    alpha_i >= 0, sum alpha_i = 1.

The required new lemma is a shipping-reachable bound

    |mu_W| <= C_phys(W) + C_att(W) + C_BA(W)
              + C_noise(W) + C_arith(W) < 1.96133 m/s^2

uniformly over the qualified A21 continuation.

This directly feeds the existing Corollary A* attitude-column floor. It does
not require pointwise AW tracking.

## 5. Stronger complete-cycle formulation

An equivalent route is to augment the physical/estimated transverse mismatch
with the LIN states and prove, over one complete scheduler cycle or service
superword,

    V_{j+1} - V_j
       <= -gamma ||Pi_perp(a_hat_w-a_phys)||_W^2
          + c_v * physical_signed_supply
          + c_b * bias_supply
          + c_n * noise/arithmetic_supply,

with gamma>0 for every covariance/tuner chronology reachable from shipping
construction.

The supply MUST retain the actual physical S mismatch; treating S=0 as a true
physical measurement would be incorrect. The useful invariant is therefore
strict passivity modulo bounded physical supply, not monotone dissipation at
each individual update.

## 8. Literal chronological lifted map

Fix one world direction `u` and first freeze the realized shipping
coefficients on one same-history word.  This is not an LTI replacement: the
coefficients below retain their actual chronological values.  Put

    z_k = [v_k, p_k, S_k, a_k]^T,

where `a_k=u' a_w,k`.  For prediction interval `h_k` with the actually
applied `tau_k`,

    z_k^- = Phi_k z_{k-1}^+,

with `Phi_k=PhiAxis4x1_analytic(tau_k,h_k)`.  Equivalently, with
`lambda_k=1/tau_k`, the last column is the exact response to an initial
acceleration,

    a(h) = exp(-lambda h) a(0),
    v(h) = v(0) + (1-exp(-lambda h))/lambda * a(0),
    p(h) = p(0) + h v(0)
           + [h/lambda-(1-exp(-lambda h))/lambda^2] a(0),
    S(h) = S(0) + h p(0) + h^2 v(0)/2
           + [h^2/(2 lambda)-h/lambda^2
              +(1-exp(-lambda h))/lambda^3] a(0).

Let `e_a=[0,0,0,1]^T` and `e_S=[0,0,1,0]^T`.
For an accepted accelerometer correction define the *actual projected
one-axis gain column*

    k^a_k = E_z K_acc,k Rhat_k u,

and retain the attitude/BA/sensor remainder `eta_k`.  The exact scalar
world innovation factorization gives

    z_k^acc
      = (I-k^a_k e_a^T) z_k^-
        + k^a_k a_phys,k + E_z K_acc,k eta_k.

For an S=0 correction, with actual gain column `k^S_k=E_z K_S,k u`,

    z_k^S = (I-k^S_k e_S^T) z_k^acc.

If both corrections occur in one cell their order is the literal source
order; no commutation is allowed.  Magnetic corrections do not directly
observe LIN, but their full-state covariance/reset action changes subsequent
`k^a,k^S` and therefore remains in the realized coefficient chronology.

Define each elementary affine operation

    z_j = A_j z_{j-1} + B_j a_phys,j + D_j eta_j.

Then for a word with operations `1,...,N`,

    z_N = M_N z_0
          + sum_{j=1}^N M_{N:j+1} B_j a_phys,j
          + sum_{j=1}^N M_{N:j+1} D_j eta_j,

    M_{r:s}=A_r A_{r-1}...A_s.

This is the requested literal chronological [a_w,v,p,S] lift.  Parameter
commits merely select the next `Phi_k,Q_k,R_S,k`; covariance synchronization
changes future gains but not the current mean directly.

For the Corollary-A* functional let `c_i^T=alpha_i e_a^T` at its sampled
epochs.  Stacking the prefix maps gives exactly

    mu_W = l_0^T z_0 + sum_j beta_j a_phys,j + sum_j d_j^T eta_j,

where

    l_0^T = sum_i c_i^T M_{i:1},
    beta_j = sum_{i>=j} c_i^T M_{i:j+1} B_j,
    d_j^T = sum_{i>=j} c_i^T M_{i:j+1} D_j.

Thus the alleged persistent nominal AW mean is not a free state trajectory:
it is a fixed signed functional of one physical acceleration history, the
carried root, and explicit defects.

## 9. Abel reduction of the physical-acceleration term

The useful bound is not `sum |beta_j| |a_j|`.  That discards exactly the
bounded-primitive information in MARINE MOTION and reproduces the failed
variation-norm route.

Let sampled physical velocity satisfy

    v^phys_j-v^phys_{j-1} = h_j a_phys,j + epsilon^quad_j,

where `epsilon^quad_j` is the jerk-controlled sampling defect.  Put
`w_j=beta_j/h_j`.  Discrete summation by parts gives

    sum_j beta_j a_phys,j
      = w_N v^phys_N - w_1 v^phys_0
        - sum_{j=1}^{N-1}(w_{j+1}-w_j) v^phys_j
        + E_jerk.

Hence

    |sum beta_j a_phys,j|
      <= V_max (|w_N|+|w_1|+TV(w))
         + |E_jerk|.

If the first velocity-level charge is still too large, apply summation by
parts once more using bounded displacement.  Writing the interior coefficient
differences as a second discrete derivative produces endpoint velocity terms
plus

    P_max * TV(Delta w / h)

and an explicitly jerk-controlled quadrature remainder.  This is the correct
place to exploit bounded displacement; it is also why a phase-locked
zero-mean wave can create some rectification but cannot automatically create
an arbitrary DC AW state.

The complete target becomes

    |l_0^T z_0|
      + C_phys(beta; V_max,P_max,J_max)
      + sum_j ||d_j|| * ||eta_j||
      < 1.96133 m/s^2.                 (AW*)

All quantities multiplying physical bounds are functions of the *reachable
shipping chronology*, not independent gain boxes.

## 10. What is proved and what remains open

The lifted identity and the Abel reduction above are exact.  They prove that
the former algebraic construction, in which innovations and AW are chosen
independently, is too relaxed: a shipping trajectory must satisfy (AW*).

They do **not yet prove the numerical strict inequality**.  The missing
uniform fact is a bound on the first/second discrete variation of the reachable
weights `beta_j` while tau, sigma_aw, r_S and T_S evolve under their coupled
scheduler and the covariance follows the literal Riccati recursion.  Existing
carried evidence (signed 16 s AW tracking-error mean <=0.371 m/s^2 on the
six audited admitted histories) is strong feasibility evidence, but is not a
source-uniform enclosure.

Therefore the next calculation is narrowly defined: export `beta_j` from the
existing literal carried-word observer for the qualified extremal histories,
compute `TV(beta/h)` and the second-difference displacement charge, and test
(AW*) before attempting rigorous interval enclosure.  Do not return to an
unsigned acceleration-variation norm and do not promote the 0.371 observation
to a theorem.
