Use only shipping-reachable hybrid entry/transition graphs and one common physical
history. Fresh Live is not the legacy independent product: held estimates and the
physical source share exact relations, while tuner, scheduler, covariance, bias
families and every literal Riccati event remain on one lineage. P3 stays fixed at
`delta=1e-18`. P4/P5 stay false until a source-uniform nonlinear certificate closes.

The active P4 architecture now matches the declared theorem objective: **bounded
accelerometer-bias error plus practical ISS/contraction of the other 18 states**.
A full 21-state A21 contraction is stronger than required and has already been
falsified at finite amplitude. The candidate backbone therefore composes every
full 21-state shipping factor first and only then selects the 18-state motion map,
so within-word motion -> b_a -> motion feedback is retained. Bias uncertainty is
charged separately through the existing same-history BIAS0/1/2 supply/projection.

## What the failures do not invalidate

They do not invalidate H18 contraction, local A21 contraction, the three bias
families, projection/Joseph graph identities, same-signal coefficient provenance,
finite-angle reset machinery, or a bounded-bias practical-stability theorem. They
are not evidence for positive BIAS2 separation, a filter change, weaker P3, or
source/domain exclusion.

## Current limiter

The controlling question is now whether the **18-state motion backbone with BA as
bounded correlated supply** remains nonlinear-contracting over the declared P4
finite-amplitude directions. Until that point feasibility is known, source-cover
and outward-LDLT tightening would be premature. If it passes, the remaining hard
work is source-uniform same-history attachment, bounded-bias supply absorption,
every-prefix retained tube, and binary32 enclosure. If it fails materially, the
3 s motion-storage architecture must also be abandoned.

point shadow reaches rho~1.10. The replacement must target the actual theorem
quantity, not improve unrelated local bounds.

1. Longer/hybrid 6/9 s motion word with every shorter prefix inside a retained
