# OU-III proof research state

## Current hypothesis

Use only shipping-reachable hybrid entry/transition graphs and one common physical
history. Fresh Live is not the legacy independent product: held estimates and the
physical source share exact relations, while tuner, scheduler, covariance, bias
families and every literal Riccati event remain on one lineage. P3 stays fixed at
`delta=1e-18`. P4/P5 stay false until a source-uniform nonlinear certificate closes.

The active candidate is now a **joint error + physical true-bias/source storage**
with consecutive compatible metric memory. The full 21-state shipping error is
retained; accelerometer bias is not required to decay as an arbitrary Cartesian
coordinate, but its physical root/driver, projection and cross terms remain in the
proof coordinate. This is the only tested architecture that simultaneously keeps
the strong A21 motion/bias cross-information and matches the declared bounded-bias
plus practical-motion-ISS theorem objective.

## Evidence and exact failure analysis

* Fresh-wrapper audit: v/p/S/aw/bg/ba estimates are held at construction values
  before first Live; `goLive()` does not generally reset already-driven linear
  means. The independent `S<=300 m*s` factor is not a fresh-Live reachability
  theorem. Shared physical/error S origin gives exact cancellation of the
  constant-origin residual while retaining physical jet/source energy.
* Same-signal period/sigma/tau/T_S/R_S relation and shipping-order frontend state
  are materialized; independent coefficient boxes remain non-promoting.
* CI fixture defects corrected without theorem changes: exact radial `[0,1]` is
  no longer outward-expanded outside its own domain; kernel/joint machines bind
  at their shared active-Riccati-schedule/geometry interface rather than equating
  unrelated auxiliary state; A21 smoke event lineage now uses estimator-owned
  `:e...` tokens.
* Complete 3 s point feasibility on one genuine same-history shipping word is
  strict in both **full linear** modes: H18 worst rho=0.9995136399194533 and A21
  worst rho=0.9959857178394912.
* **Full-product A21 nonlinear failure:** exact-Cayley frozen-gain shadow first
  crosses rho=1 at scale 5.5 and reaches about 1.1031 in the full-Phi metric,
  dominated by accelerometer-bias error. Classification: proof-formulation /
  regional-basin failure, not demonstrated shipping instability. Refinement of
  that same full-product 3 s storage is forbidden.
* **Motion18 candidate failure:** using the exact map/covariance artifact from the
  successful complete-word run, the 18x18 principal motion map remains strict in
  H18 (rho=0.9995136399194534) but A21 reaches **rho=10.119172378460503** at
  t0=1192.00732421875 s even with the root accelerometer-bias perturbation set
  exactly to zero. The maximizing root is motion-only (ba tail exactly zero).
  Classification: theorem/storage architecture failure, not enclosure failure.
  It invalidates the idea that the A21 moving **marginal motion covariance** can
  be used as the 3 s P4 storage with bias charged only as an external supply.
  The motion18 scripts/workflow were removed instead of retained as dead tooling.
* The cause is structural: discarding covariance cross-information is catastrophic
  in A21. On the same exact trace, retaining the **full 21-state endpoint
  precision** while restricting only the root perturbation to the 18-state motion
  subspace gives worst A21 rho about **0.9398859970**. Thus the information carried
  by motion/bias cross terms is essential.
* Existing 24-state joint error/physical-bias compatible-storage evidence is
  correspondingly promising: point compatible-storage product rates are
  H18=0.9996524355874086 and A21=0.9959531012474646. The one-correlated-root
  retention diagnostic is retained at one sigma and reports critical initial
  levels H18=4.4551206340521015 sigma (limiting velocity) and
  A21=112.42280878895119 sigma (limiting latent acceleration).
* The same joint-storage point experiment also exposes the remaining issue: its
  old independent coordinate-prefix capacity is not retained (reported maximum
  prefix ratios about 39.65 H18 and 15.43 A21). That does **not** invalidate the
  compatible metric; it shows the prefix tube must be built from the reachable
  correlated source/entry graph rather than the historical product box.

## What the failures do not invalidate

They do not invalidate H18 contraction, the three bias families, projection/Joseph
identities, same-signal coefficient provenance, finite-angle reset machinery, the
joint physical-bias recurrence, or a bounded-bias practical-stability theorem.
They are not evidence for positive BIAS2 separation, a filter change, weaker P3,
or source/domain exclusion.

## Current limiter

The controlling task is now **source-uniform reachable-prefix retention for the
joint 24-state error/true-bias coordinate with compatible metric memory**. Point
contraction exists. What is missing is the universal bridge from the corrected
fresh-Live/BRMM source graph into that storage, with nonzero BIAS0/1/2 drivers,
active projection, source-dependent estimator/Riccati schedule, nonlinear reset,
finite precision, and every literal prefix kept inside a hard tube.

## Alternatives and critic pass

The 18-state marginal metric is abandoned because its exact point rho is ~10.1;
no interval or subdivision improvement can cross that gap. The strongest reason
for the joint-storage route is that keeping the missing cross-information restores
strict point rates without changing the filter or shrinking the physical domain.

If joint reachable-prefix retention fails quantitatively, the remaining distinct
alternatives are:

1. longer/hybrid 6/9 s joint word with a separately certified shorter-prefix tube;
2. a different joint `(e_ba,beta_true)` cross-term metric optimized against the
   source recurrence rather than the current compatible point metric;
3. source-dependent metric memory with a stronger consecutive compatibility
   inequality across estimator/Riccati cells;
4. H18->A21 reachable induction from the exact unlock graph rather than an
   arbitrary A21 product entrance.
