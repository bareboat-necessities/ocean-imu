# Source-uniform reachable-graph theorem for adaptive shaped storage

Governing rule: `docs/ou3-shipping-faithfulness-protocol.md`.
This replaces finite-replay quantification by quantification over the complete
shipping-reachable same-history class.  It does not assert assembled-device
qualification of conditional SLOW+FAST constants.

## RG1. One history, one causal state

Let h denote one admitted infinite physical/delivered-sensor history satisfying
the theorem's MARINE MOTION, MAGNETIC SERVICE and conditional SLOW+FAST
contracts.  Let X_k(h) be the complete literal shipping state immediately
before sample k: estimator mean and covariance; quaternion/reference and
magnetic-reference state; BA/BG state and gates; frontend Mahony state;
frequency/period/variance/bandpass state; applied and staged tuner state;
S scheduler elapsed phase; covariance-sync phase; reset/watchdog state; and
the carried physical/SLOW/FAST primitives.

The shipping source defines a deterministic causal transition

    X_{k+1} = F_ship(X_k,y_k,m_k,h_k).

Define R_k as the image of all admitted histories under this literal recursion,
starting from the certified construction/capture/release class.  This image,
not a Cartesian product of marginal ranges, is the admissible coefficient set.

## RG2. Generated storage graph

For X in R_k let tau(X), sigma(X) be the applied shipping tuple and D(X) the
exact OU/S normalization.  Define

    Pbar(X)=D(X) P(X) D(X)',
    Q(X)=Pbar(X)^-1,
    W(X,e)=e_bar' Q(X) e_bar.

The LIN block is fully coupled.  Q is not selected independently: it is a
deterministic function on the same reachable state X that generates every gain,
R_S,T_S and scheduler event.

For any literal mean operation j on the same history,

    ebar_{j+1}=Abar_j(X_j)e_bar_j+dbar_j(X_j,h),

and therefore exactly

    W_{j+1}-W_j
      = ebar_j'[Abar_j' Q(X_{j+1}) Abar_j-Q(X_j)]ebar_j
        +2 ebar_j' Abar_j'Q(X_{j+1})dbar_j
        +dbar_j'Q(X_{j+1})dbar_j.                         (RG1)

RG1 is an identity on the shipping reachable graph.  It preserves source/loss
direction and tuner/covariance correlation.  No ||b||^2/lambda_min relaxation
has occurred.

## RG3. Superword accumulation

For a physical superword W=[k,k+N], sum RG1 before taking any norm. Internal
storage terms telescope:

    W_N-W_0 = -D_W(e_0;h)+S_W(e_0;h),                    (RG2)

where D_W is the signed homogeneous quadratic action generated by the literal
prediction/correction/reset sequence and S_W contains the prospectively
composed local physical/model/reset defects on that SAME h.

The existing qualitative theorem gives D_W(e;h)>0 for every nonzero homogeneous
root on qualified MOVING superwords; compact release/reachable classes imply
that a quantitative source-uniform theorem reduces to proving a positive
minimum of the RATIO on the reachable graph, not on an enlarged box:

    alpha_* = inf_{h,e: W(X_0,e)=1} D_W(e;h) > 0,          (RG3)
    E_*     = sup_{h,e: W(X_0,e)<=C} S_W(e;h) < infinity. (RG4)

Continuity plus compactness proves existence once the exact reachable graph over
the chosen superword is compact and the zero set has been excluded.  This is
the correct topological upgrade from the already-proved qualitative strictness.
A useful numerical radius still requires constructive values of alpha_* and
E_*; existence alone does not prove entry into 0.15.

## RG4. Why alpha_* is positive conditionally

Assume the already-recorded compact outer A21 release region and literal
finite-horizon continuity/totality.  Take a 60/100-s superword long enough to
contain the invoked MARINE/SLOW+FAST/MAGNETIC mechanisms.  Suppose alpha_*=0.
Compactness yields a convergent sequence (h_n,e_n) in the reachable graph with
unit W and D_W->0. Continuity gives a reachable limit with unit W and zero
homogeneous action. The established complete-word kernel <=1 and persistent
A*/MARINE/MAGNETIC exclusion on qualified MOVING superwords force that limit
root to zero, contradicting unit W. Hence alpha_*>0.

This is lossless at the shipping execution level, conditional on the existing
compactness and qualification premises. It uses rotating weak directions over
the complete superword rather than one-word lambda_min.

## RG5. Physical supply is a continuous functional of the same history

Each local defect is generated prospectively by the literal operation.  On the
conditional SLOW+FAST class, slow terms use the carried rate/amplitude history
and fast terms use their signed primitives without reset at W boundaries.
Physical acceleration terms telescope through bounded v,p,S before norms.
Tuner/Mahony terms are not external disturbances: they are coordinates of X(h)
and already enter Abar,Q and scheduler chronology.  Thus S_W is a continuous
functional on the same compact reachable history graph. It has a finite
supremum E_*; no independent source/gain product is needed.

This proves existence of a finite source-uniform shaped-storage absorbing radius
conditionally. It does NOT yet prove that radius enters V<0.15^2. The remaining
constructive obligation is a dependency-preserving enclosure of the linked
ratio E_*/alpha_* (and every prefix) on R_W.

## RG6. Constructive certificate target

The certificate must cover the reachable graph by causal history cells. A cell
contains one shared physical/sensor-history enclosure propagated through:
Mahony/frontend -> frequency/variance -> staged tuner -> tau,sigma,R_S,T_S ->
literal covariance/gains -> OU/S mean factors.  Branch-and-bound may subdivide
the HISTORY INPUT cell; it may not independently subdivide generated outputs.

For each cell compute interval/affine enclosures of the pair

    (D_W(e;h), S_W(e;h))

with common dependencies retained, and solve the generalized linked quotient

    sup S_W / D_W

only after zero-action directions are removed by the already-proved
superword kernel/A* theorem.  Prefix cells are propagated from the same root.

Promotion target:

    W_N <= rho W_0 + E_phys, rho<1,
    E_phys/(1-rho) < W_entry,

plus every-prefix retention and a proved comparison W_entry -> V<0.15^2.

## Shipping-faithfulness audit

Structures preserved: all literal mean/covariance/gain chronology; Mahony and
frequency/variance adaptation; jointly generated tau,sigma,R_S,T_S; exact OU/S
chain; S=0; MARINE; MAGNETIC SERVICE; SLOW+FAST histories; BA/reset/reference
chronology; source/loss correlation; rotating weak directions; persistent state
across word boundaries.

Relaxations introduced: none in RG1--RG5 beyond conditioning on the theorem's
declared physical qualification and already-recorded compact release premises.
RG6's finite cover is a proof implementation, not an enlarged execution family,
provided subdivision occurs only in shared history inputs.

Failure classes: inability to obtain a useful constructive quotient is D.
A cover implementation/parity error is E. Only a literal admitted history that
violates the theorem is A.
