# Joint SLOW+FAST physical gauge separation

This note fixes the quantifiers for the controlling physical ambiguity test. It
does not add a runtime assumption, a frequency cutoff, an independent residual
box, or numerical qualification.

For one complete physical word W, let X_W be the set of PAIRS of continuous
physical histories satisfying the same declared MARINE MOTION and MAGNETIC
SERVICE contracts and producing the same accepted magnetic service pattern.
The pair carries its actual attitudes, p/v/a/S primitives, delivered epochs,
and one persistent SLOW+FAST decomposition for each sensor.

For a pair x=(x0,x1), define the exact delivered compatibility defects

    c_a,k(x) = R_1,k' (a_1,k-g_1,k) - R_0,k' (a_0,k-g_0,k),
    c_g,k(x) = omega_1,k-omega_0,k.

Let Delta E_a(q_a|past) and Delta E_g(q_g|past) be the differences of two
reachable SLOW+FAST histories on those SAME delivered cells, with
q_i=(H_i,C_i), inherited B_i,s,D_i,s,B_i,f, all predecessor-linked slow
increments, and every placed fast-window constraint retained. No endpoint box
or reset of the fast primitive is substituted.

A pair is sensor compatible iff simultaneously

    c_a(x) in Delta E_a(q_a|past),
    c_g(x) in Delta E_g(q_g|past).                         (JG1)

The magnetic condition is not a third independent norm bound: x must also lie
in the actual applied MAGNETIC SERVICE compatibility class. In particular a
constant relative rotation about a constant world magnetic field can have
c_g=0; gyro and magnetic service alone therefore do not break that gauge.

For any literal causal test operator L_W built from the actual interleaved
prediction/S/accelerometer/magnetic chronology, define the JOINT separation

    delta_W(q_a,q_g)
      = inf_{x in X_W, x nontrivial}
        inf_{u_a in Delta E_a(q_a|past),
             u_g in Delta E_g(q_g|past)}
        || L_W [c_a(x)-u_a ; c_g(x)-u_g] ||.               (JG2)

The two sensor residuals are stacked before the norm. The coefficients of L_W
are generated by the SAME physical/filter history; they may not be optimized
independently. SF5/SF6 may be used to evaluate support functions of the two
reachable bias sets, but process and accelerometer columns must be paired before
norms and internal p/v/S/a slab endpoints must telescope first.

For a fixed qualified MARINE tuple

    m=(T_E,theta_E,T_P,P_E)

and the existing magnetic constants, the exact assembled-device qualification
region required by gauge breaking is

    Q_IMU(m) = {(H_a,C_a,H_g,C_g):
                 H_a>0, 0<=C_a<B_a,f H_a,
                 H_g>0, 0<=C_g<B_g,f H_g,
                 inf_{admitted complete W} delta_W > 0}.   (JG3)

Thus (JG3), not separate accelerometer and gyro witness thresholds, is the
source-uniform gauge-breaking condition. Once m is numerically fixed, JG3 is
the requested four-dimensional IMU region. On current main T_E,theta_E,T_P,P_E
are also OPEN, so the presently honest conditional region is

    Q_phys = {(m,q_a,q_g): MARINE(m) is qualified and (JG3) holds}.            (JG4)

The old phi=.01 sin(t/2) inequalities remain necessary witness-exclusion tests
only; they are not JG3.

The PR #640 paired two-Abel construction is the intended literal realization
of L_W for the outer field-axis bridge. The missing executable input was the
aligned physical p/v/S/a lift at every literal operation boundary. The read-only
exporter work on PR #641 supplies that lift for fixed carried words; replay
values remain diagnostics. Source-uniform closure still requires a rigorous
factor/interval enclosure of JG2 over the compact reachable outer class.

Held-H18 LIN BIBO and captured-domain release compactness are already closed
qualitatively by the post-#640 certificates. They are downstream of physical
capture and do not replace JG3.
