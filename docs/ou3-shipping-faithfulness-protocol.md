## Failure classification -- exactly one class per failed attempt

**A. SHIPPING COUNTEREXAMPLE.** A complete execution satisfies literal shipping estimator, covariance, adaptation, tuner, OU/S, scheduler, reset and BA equations; MARINE MOTION; MAGNETIC SERVICE; SLOW+FAST IMU assumptions; physical bounds; and every theorem premise, yet violates the theorem conclusion. Only A directly refutes the shipping theorem by counterexample.

**B. LOSSLESS SHIPPING-LEVEL IMPOSSIBILITY.** A theorem derived directly from complete shipping equations, without enlarging the execution family, proves the desired property impossible. B can refute the theorem.

**C. RELAXED-MODEL COUNTEREXAMPLE.** A witness exists only after dropping a shipping coupling, independently varying coupled quantities, replacing temporal constraints by pointwise boxes, deleting adaptation, or otherwise enlarging the reachable family. It refutes only that relaxation.

**D. SUFFICIENT-BOUND FAILURE.** A conservative sufficient inequality fails but no violating shipping execution is produced. It refutes only the proof technique.

**E. IMPLEMENTATION/DIAGNOSTIC FAILURE.** Instrumentation, replay, arithmetic, CI or certificate implementation is incomplete or wrong. It proves nothing mathematical until repaired.

Never describe C, D or E as filter instability.
